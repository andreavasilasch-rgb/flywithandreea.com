Good to know

Private jet route questions.

How much does it cost to charter a private jet?+

Private jet charter is priced per flight, not per seat. As a guide, a light jet on a short European hop starts from around EUR 7,000 one-way, a midsize jet from roughly EUR 15,000, and a heavy or ultra-long-range jet for intercontinental sectors from EUR 30,000 upward. The final price depends on the route, aircraft category, aircraft positioning and season. Open any route below for an indicative from-price, or message us for a firm quote.

What are the most popular private jet routes?+

Some of the most requested routes are London to Nice, Dubai to London, Geneva to Ibiza, Paris to Milan and Zurich to Nice, along with Gulf sectors such as Dubai to Riyadh and Doha to London. Our directory covers more than 26,000 city pairs across 180 countries, so you can search any origin and destination and open a dedicated page with airports, aircraft, flight time and pricing.

How do I book a private jet for a specific route?+

Find your route using the search and filters, open its page, and send us a message on WhatsApp or request a quote. We confirm the best aircraft for your party and schedule, the departure and arrival FBOs, timing and a firm all-in price. There is no account to create and no obligation to book.

How far in advance should I book a private jet?+

Private jets can often be arranged within a few hours for popular routes, and we regularly handle same-day departures. For peak periods, larger groups or long-range aircraft, booking two to three days ahead widens the choice of aircraft and pricing. Tell us your dates and we will confirm availability quickly.

Is a one-way private jet cheaper as an empty leg?+

It can be. When an aircraft repositions one-way it is sometimes offered as an empty leg at a fraction of the standard charter price. If your route and dates line up with an available repositioning flight, the saving can be 25 to 75 percent. Browse current empty legs or set up an alert for the routes you fly most.

Which aircraft is right for my route?+

Short hops up to roughly 1,500 km suit a light jet; sectors up to about 3,500 km a midsize or super-midsize; longer routes a heavy jet; and intercontinental flights an ultra-long-range jet that flies non-stop. Each route page recommends suitable aircraft with cabin and range details, and you can filter this directory by aircraft category.

How long does a private jet flight take compared with commercial?+

The flight time itself is similar, but private travel removes the hours around it. You arrive minutes before departure at a private terminal, skip check-in and security queues, fly point-to-point without connections, and clear a dedicated arrivals facility. Each route page lists the typical block time for that sector.

Do you cover private jet routes worldwide?+

Yes. Fly with Andreea arranges charters across Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia-Pacific and the Americas, working with more than 450 vetted operators. This directory lists over 26,000 routes and 180 countries; if you do not see your exact city pair, message us and we will arrange it.
