Private jet routes, questions and answers.

How much does it cost to charter a private jet?

Private jet charter is priced per flight, not per seat. As a guide, a light jet on a European flight of up to 90 minutes starts from around €8,000 to €11,000 one way, a midsize jet on a four hour sector from roughly €32,000, a super midsize jet on longer sectors from around €35,000, and a heavy or ultra long range jet for intercontinental sectors from €72,000 upward. The final price depends on the route, aircraft category, aircraft positioning and season. Open any route for an indicative from-price, or message us for a firm quote.

What are the most popular private jet routes?

Some of the most requested routes are London to Nice, Dubai to London, Geneva to Ibiza, Paris to Milan and Zurich to Nice, along with Gulf sectors such as Dubai to Riyadh and Doha to London. Our directory covers more than 26,000 city pairs across 180 countries.

How do I book a private jet for a specific route?

Find your route using the search and filters, open its page, and request a quote. We confirm the best aircraft for your party and schedule, the departure and arrival terminals, timing and a firm total price. There is no account to create and no obligation to book.

How far in advance should I book a private jet?

Private jets can often be arranged within a few hours for popular routes, and we regularly handle same-day departures. For peak periods, larger groups or long-range aircraft, booking two to three days ahead widens the choice of aircraft and pricing.

Is a one-way private jet cheaper as an empty leg?

It can be. When an aircraft repositions one way it is sometimes offered as an empty leg at a fraction of the standard charter price. If your route and dates line up with an available repositioning flight, the saving can be significant. Browse current empty legs or ask us for the routes you fly most.

Which aircraft is right for my route?

We only propose aircraft that fly your route non-stop. Flights up to about 2,700 km suit a light jet, up to about 3,500 km a midsize jet, up to about 5,400 km a super midsize jet, up to about 6,600 km a heavy jet, and longer sectors an ultra long range jet. Each route in this directory shows the smallest category that flies it non-stop, and each route page recommends suitable aircraft with cabin and range details.

How long does a private jet flight take compared with commercial?

The flight time itself is similar, but private travel removes the hours around it. You arrive minutes before departure at a private terminal, skip check-in and security queues, fly point to point and clear a dedicated arrivals facility.

Do you cover private jet routes worldwide?

Yes. Fly with Andreea arranges charters across Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia-Pacific and the Americas. This directory lists over 26,000 routes in 180 countries; if you do not see your exact city pair, message us and we will arrange it.
