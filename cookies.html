Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Fly with Andreea uses cookies and similar technologies on flywithandreea.com, and how you can control them.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used.

2. Cookies we use

Strictly necessary — required for the website to work, such as remembering your language and enabling core features. These cannot be switched off.

Preference — remember choices you make, such as your selected language, to improve your experience.

Analytics — where enabled, help us understand aggregate, anonymised usage so we can improve the site. We do not use these to identify you personally.

3. Location detection

We may detect your approximate country from your IP address to show relevant content and the correct language. This is processed without storing personal data for that purpose.

4. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and set your browser to refuse cookies. Some features of the site may not function correctly if you disable strictly necessary cookies.

5. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

6. More information

For how we handle personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
