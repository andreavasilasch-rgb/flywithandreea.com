India

India is flying. And it is a joy to watch.

The largest charter fleet in Asia, a cinema industry the whole world is watching, and cities close enough to string four into a single day. Here is how private jet charter India works in practice, route by route, and what it feels like.

The moment

A release the size of a country.

On 26 August, Toxic: A Fairy Tale for Grown-Ups opened in cinemas worldwide. It is Yash's return to the screen after KGF: Chapter 2, directed by Geetu Mohandas, with Kiara Advani and Rukmini Vasanth, released simultaneously in Kannada, English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Malayalam.

Six languages on one day is a remarkable thing to pull off, and it says something lovely about where Indian cinema now sits: not a regional industry with an export arm, but a genuinely pan-Indian one that plays everywhere at once.

Bengaluru in the morning, Chennai by lunch, Hyderabad for the evening, Mumbai the same night. India is one of the few places where that is a normal ambition.

That is also, quietly, the story of private jet charter India. Not luxury for its own sake, but compression of time. A day that a timetable cannot hold, held anyway.

India operates roughly 139 business jets, about 248 helicopters and 102 turboprops. Together that is the largest charter fleet in Asia, and business jet registrations rose about 35% in 2024.

The wider Indian aviation market is valued at around 14.78 billion dollars in 2025, growing towards 16.53 billion in 2026, with compound growth of about 11.9%. For anyone who loves this industry, watching a market of that scale mature is genuinely exciting.

Why the sector suits a private aircraft

Private jet charter India begins, for most clients, with a long sector. A European departure is a long day whichever way it is flown. What changes on a private aircraft is not the distance but the shape of the day. You depart from the airport that suits you rather than the one with the convenient slot, you arrive at a private terminal at both ends, and the hours in between are yours rather than borrowed.

The Gulf sectors are a different pleasure entirely. Two and a half hours from Abu Dhabi to Mumbai is a morning, not a journey. It is the kind of distance that makes a same-day return genuinely sensible, and it is one of the most requested pairings we see between the Gulf and the subcontinent.

Ahmedabad deserves a particular mention. It is comfortably the best connected Indian city across our route network, which means the pricing and the aircraft availability on those pairings are unusually well understood before anyone picks up a phone.

Choosing the cabin

Which private jet for which sector.

The right aircraft for India depends almost entirely on the leg. A seven hour sector from Europe wants an ultra long range cabin. A two hour hop from the Gulf is a very different brief, and paying for range you will not use is simply money left on the tarmac.

How long is a private jet flight from London to Delhi?

About 6,720 km, roughly 3,627 nautical miles, which is close to seven hours fifty minutes in an ultra long range aircraft. It is flown non-stop.

Can a private jet fly from Europe to India non-stop?

Yes, in an ultra long range aircraft. The Bombardier Global 7500 is certified to 8,890 nautical miles, and London to Delhi is about 3,627, so the sector is flown direct with a generous margin for winds and alternates.

Which aircraft suits a Europe to India flight best?

An ultra long range type such as the Global 7500, which carries up to nineteen passengers and offers four separate living spaces. For Gulf to India sectors of two to three hours, a heavy or super midsize cabin is a very comfortable fit.

How long is the flight from Dubai or Abu Dhabi to Mumbai?

Abu Dhabi to Mumbai is about 1,970 km, roughly 1,064 nautical miles, around two hours thirty five minutes. Dubai to Mumbai is very similar. These are short, easy sectors that suit a wide range of cabins.

Which airports do private jets use in Mumbai?

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International, BOM, has a dedicated general aviation terminal used by business aviation, and Juhu Aerodrome nearby serves helicopters and smaller aircraft for onward transfers into the city.

Which airports do private jets use in Delhi and Bengaluru?

Indira Gandhi International, DEL, is India's busiest airport for private aviation, with an expanded general aviation terminal planned. Bengaluru is served by Kempegowda International, BLR, the natural arrival point for the south.

How large is private aviation in India?

India operates roughly 139 business jets alongside about 248 helicopters and 102 turboprops, together the largest charter fleet in Asia. Business jet registrations rose about 35% in 2024.

Can a private jet do several Indian cities in one day?

Yes, and it is one of the most common reasons people charter here. Mumbai to Delhi is about one hour forty, Mumbai to Bengaluru about one hour twenty. Several cities in a single day is comfortably achievable with the right aircraft and operator arranged in advance.

Who operates the aircraft?

Are there empty leg flights on India routes?

They appear from time to time on long range aircraft repositioning between Europe, the Gulf and Asia. Because the sectors are long, the value when one matches your dates is considerable. Live availability is listed on the empty legs page.

How much luggage can travel on a private jet to India?

Private cabins are generous compared with commercial flights, and an ultra long range aircraft has substantial hold volume. Tell us the bags, camera equipment or golf clubs when you enquire and the capacity is confirmed for that specific aircraft.

Can pets travel to India on a private jet?

Pets usually travel in the cabin beside you rather than in cargo. India applies its own documentation requirements for animals, which we prepare with the operator ahead of departure.

What documents are needed for a private flight to India?

Valid passports and the appropriate Indian visa for every passenger, together with the crew and aircraft documentation the operator files for permissions. Passenger details are collected early because those permissions depend on them.

How far ahead should an Indian charter be arranged?

The earlier the better, because international permissions are filed in advance and the choice of cabin is widest. Shorter notice is often possible, and the fastest way to find out is simply to ask.
