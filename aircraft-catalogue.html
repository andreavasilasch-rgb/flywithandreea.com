Can I charter any aircraft in this catalogue?
Yes. Every aircraft shown is accessible through our network of 450+ vetted operators. Tell Andreea the aircraft or category you have in mind and she arranges it end to end.
How do I choose the right private jet?
It comes down to passengers, distance and luggage. Light and very light jets suit short European hops; midsize and super-midsize add range and cabin comfort; heavy and long-range jets fly intercontinental non-stop. Andreea recommends the right one for your route.
What is the difference between light, midsize and heavy jets?
Light jets carry 4–7 passengers on shorter sectors; midsize and super-midsize seat 7–9 with transcontinental range and stand-up cabins; heavy and long-range jets seat 10–19 for intercontinental travel with full galleys and private suites.
How much does it cost to charter each aircraft?
Pricing is quoted per route, fixed and all-in, with no hidden fees. Costs rise with cabin size and range. Request a quote and Andreea returns a clear price within minutes.
How far can a private jet fly without refuelling?
It depends on the category. Light jets typically cover 1,500 to 3,000 km, midsize and super-midsize jets 3,000 to 6,000 km, and heavy and ultra-long-range jets fly 10,000 to 14,000 km non-stop, enough for Dubai to New York or London to Singapore. Every aircraft in this catalogue lists its exact range in kilometres and miles.
How many passengers can a private jet carry?
The private jets in our fleet seat from 4 passengers on a very light jet up to 19 on ultra-long-range jets and VIP airliners. Most light and midsize jets carry 6 to 9 travellers in full comfort. Each aircraft card shows its exact passenger capacity so you can match the jet to your group.
How fast do private jets fly?
Most private jets cruise between 700 and 900 km/h. Long-range and heavy jets are the fastest, often close to Mach 0.90. The cruise speed of every aircraft is listed on its card, so you can compare block times and choose the quickest jet for your route.
Which private jet is best for transatlantic and long-haul flights?
For transatlantic and intercontinental travel, choose a heavy or ultra-long-range jet such as the Gulfstream G650, Bombardier Global 7500 or Dassault Falcon 8X. These aircraft fly non-stop between continents with stand-up cabins, private suites and full galleys. Andreea will recommend the right long-range jet for your route.
Are private jet charters safe?
Yes. Every flight is operated by one of 450+ vetted operators that meet strict international safety and maintenance standards. Andreea selects the operator for each leg, so you travel only with fully certified aircraft and experienced crews.
Can I book a private jet at short notice?
Yes. With 450+ operators worldwide, most private jet charters can be arranged within hours, and take-off is possible in as little as 55 minutes when an aircraft is positioned nearby. Message Andreea with your route and dates for a fast, fixed, all-in quote.
