This leg flies from Marka (OJAM) in Jordan to Cairo International (HECA) in Egypt, 261 nm. Departure is set by the operator's schedule for 15 Nov 2026 and can move by a few hours, so confirm before planning around it.

Because the aircraft is repositioning anyway, the whole cabin goes for a fraction of the same routing chartered from scratch. You are not buying a seat. You are buying the aircraft.

How empty leg flights work

When a private jet flies one way for a client it often has to return or reposition with nobody on board. Those legs are offered at a reduced rate. The trade is flexibility: the date, the route and the aircraft are already decided, and a leg can be withdrawn if the original trip changes.
