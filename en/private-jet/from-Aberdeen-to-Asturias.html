Good to know

Aberdeen to Asturias. Everything you need to know.

How much does it cost to charter a private jet from Aberdeen to Asturias?+
Charter pricing between Aberdeen (ABZ) and Asturias (OVD) typically ranges from €9,500 to €20,000 for a light or midsize jet, reflecting the 1,800 km sector and Spain's competitive private aviation market. Key pricing variables include aircraft category and group size, seasonal peaks (August and September when Asturian demand is highest), aircraft positioning costs from Scotland, landing and handling fees at Asturias Airport, in-flight catering for the two-hour journey, and crew overnight expenses for multi-day itineraries. For larger groups requiring a super-midsize or large-cabin aircraft, rates rise to €16,000 to €28,000. Fly with Andreea provides fully transparent, all-inclusive quotations.
What airports serve private jet travel between Aberdeen and Asturias?+
Aberdeen Airport (ABZ / EGPD) handles UK departures with general aviation facilities and private customs. Asturias Airport (OVD / LEAS), also known as Oviedo Airport, is the destination serving the region 40 km north-west of Oviedo city and 7 km from Avilés. OVD handles private jet movements through its general aviation apron with local handling agents and has a runway of 3,400 m suitable for midsize and large-cabin jets. The airport is located in a valley with specific instrument approach characteristics, making experienced crew knowledge of the airfield valuable all aircraft sourced through Fly with Andreea are operated by crews qualified for OVD's approach procedures. Santander Airport (SDR / LEXJ) is an alternative for clients based in eastern Asturias or Cantabria, approximately 120 km east.
Which private jets are most suitable for the Aberdeen to Asturias route?+
The 1,800 km Aberdeen–Asturias sector is comfortably within light jet range, though midsize aircraft are popular for groups preferring a stand-up cabin on the two-hour flight. The Embraer Phenom 300E leads demand on Spanish Atlantic routes from Scotland in 2025, combining a nine-passenger cabin, 3,650 km range, and strong short-field performance. The Cessna Citation CJ4 is a proven light-jet option for groups of four to six, with the range and reliability expected on routine Scotland-Spain sectors. For Camino de Santiago expedition groups carrying significant kit walking poles, boots, specialty packs the Pilatus PC-12 NG's large rear cargo door and flexible cabin configuration provides the most practical solution. A Fly with Andreea advisor will recommend the right aircraft for your group size, luggage, and seasonal requirements.
What is the typical private jet flight time between Aberdeen and Asturias?+
Aberdeen to Asturias covers approximately 1,800 km, with typical block times of two hours to two hours thirty minutes for light and midsize jets. Routing tracks south over the UK and France before crossing the Bay of Biscay into northern Spain. Passengers using Aberdeen's FBO arrive 15 to 20 minutes before departure no shared security lanes, no check-in desks, no commercial boarding queues. At Asturias Airport, private customs clearance is quick and vehicles are waiting at the aircraft steps, with Oviedo's historic centre 45 minutes by road, Gijón's harbour 20 minutes, and the Picos de Europa national park entrance at Cangas de Onís approximately one hour.
Are there night flight restrictions at Asturias Airport for private jets?+
Asturias Airport (OVD) does not impose a formal published night curfew for general aviation operations, but movements outside standard published hours should be confirmed with the ground handling agent in advance. The airport is subject to specific approach and departure procedures owing to its mountainous terrain setting, which can restrict operations in low-visibility or strong crosswind conditions Fly with Andreea's crew selection for this route accounts for OVD airfield qualifications. Aberdeen Airport (ABZ) has no night curfew for business aviation. Fly with Andreea's operations team books handling slots, monitors weather and ATC conditions at OVD, and maintains filed alternate airports typically Santander or Bilbao as contingency options for weather-affected arrivals.

Upon landing at Asturias Airport (OVD), Fly with Andreea coordinates chauffeur-driven transfers in executive vehicles. For Camino de Santiago clients beginning the Camino Primitivo at Oviedo Cathedral, we coordinate full kit-and-luggage transfers to your designated pilgrim hotel. How much luggage can I bring on a private jet from Aberdeen to Asturias?+
Light jets such as the Phenom 300E or Citation CJ4 accommodate four to seven full-size suitcases plus cabin bags sufficient for most group sizes on a one-week Asturian itinerary. For Camino de Santiago walkers or Picos de Europa trekking groups with substantial specialist kit, the Pilatus PC-12 NG is the optimal solution, with its large rear cargo door handling walking boot bags, trekking poles, tent systems, and photography equipment efficiently. For clients arriving with wine-buying samples, Asturian cider cases, or art acquisitions from Oviedo's galleries, Fly with Andreea's team will confirm the appropriate hold configuration in advance. There are no commercial-style baggage fees, no weight restrictions, and no shared baggage handling.
Can I bring my dog or cat on a private jet from Aberdeen to Asturias?+
Your beloved companions are welcome in the cabin with you for this two-hour Atlantic crossing. Spain, as an EU member state, applies the EU Pet Travel Scheme. Required documentation includes: an animal health certificate (AHC) completed by a UK Official Veterinarian (OV) within 10 days of travel; a valid microchip conforming to ISO 11784/11785; a current rabies vaccination administered at least 21 days before first EU entry; and for dogs, an Echinococcus (tapeworm) treatment certificate issued between 24 and 120 hours before arrival. Asturias Airport's general aviation area processes private-flight pet arrivals routinely. Fly with Andreea will provide the current AHC template and connect you with an OV in your area. We recommend initiating documentation at least 72 hours before departure.
