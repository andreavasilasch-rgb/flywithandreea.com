How Much Does It Cost to Charter a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Accra?+
Chartering a private jet from Abu Dhabi to Accra typically costs between approximately $85,000 and $160,000 for a one-way flight, depending on aircraft category. At 6,200 km, this route exceeds the range of light and midsize jets, making a large-cabin jet such as the Gulfstream G450 or Bombardier Global 5000 the practical minimum , these start from around $85,000. Ultra-long-range jets capable of flying the sector non-stop, such as the Gulfstream G650ER or Global 7500, command a premium from $130,000 to $160,000+. Key pricing variables include aircraft positioning and repositioning fees, overflight permit costs across multiple airspaces, seasonal demand fluctuations, fuel surcharges, and any technical-stop handling fees if a mid-route refuelling is required.
Which Airports Serve Private Jet Flights Between Abu Dhabi and Accra?+
For private departures from Abu Dhabi, the preferred facility is Al Bateen Executive Airport (AZI / OMAD), a dedicated business-aviation airport located 8 km from the city centre. Al Bateen hosts four FBOs and consistently offers faster processing and less congestion than the main Zayed International Airport (AUH / OMAA), though AUH can accommodate ultra-large aircraft and intercontinental operations when required. In Accra, the sole international gateway is Accra International Airport , formerly Kotoka International Airport , (ACC / DGAA), located approximately 9 km north-east of the city centre. The airport operates a modern Terminal 3 for international arrivals and has dedicated general-aviation handling providers able to manage VIP private jet arrivals and departures, with a 3,400 m runway accommodating wide-body and large-cabin aircraft with ease.
Which Aircraft Models Are Best Suited for Private Jet Flights from Abu Dhabi to Accra?+
The Abu Dhabi, Accra route demands aircraft with sufficient range and cabin comfort for a flight of 7 to 9 hours. Three frequently chartered options are:

1. Gulfstream G450 (Large Jet) , Range 7,800 km; can complete the sector with one technical stop; seats up to 14 passengers in a stand-up cabin with full galley.
2. Bombardier Global 5000 (Large/Ultra-Long-Range Jet) , Range 9,600 km; comfortably handles the route non-stop; features three distinct cabin zones and a full-size galley.
3. Gulfstream G650ER (Ultra-Long-Range Jet) , Range 12,960 km; the benchmark for intercontinental non-stop comfort; seats up to 19 passengers in an ultra-wide-body cabin.

A dedicated Private Aviation Advisor can help you select the right aircraft to match your passenger count, luggage requirements, and budget.
What Is the Typical Flight Duration for a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Accra?+
The great-circle distance between Abu Dhabi (AZI/AUH) and Accra (ACC) is approximately 6,200 km. On a non-stop ultra-long-range jet such as the Global 5000 or Gulfstream G650ER, the flight time is typically 7 hours 30 minutes to 8 hours 30 minutes depending on winds and routing. If a technical stop for refuelling is required on a large-cabin jet (e.g. G450), total block time including the ground stop is approximately 10 to 11 hours. Private aviation's core advantage on this sector is access to dedicated FBO terminals: at Al Bateen, passengers can arrive as little as 20 minutes before departure, bypassing commercial airport queues entirely. Both departure and arrival handling can include expedited customs and immigration clearance, chauffeured ramp-side transfers, and private lounge access , making the door-to-door journey significantly faster than any commercial alternative.
Are There Night Flight Restrictions on the Abu Dhabi, Accra Route?+
Al Bateen Executive Airport (AZI / OMAD) operates 24 hours a day with no curfew, making it an ideal departure point for overnight or early-morning departures on this long-haul sector. Zayed International Airport (AUH / OMAA) also operates on a 24-hour basis, accommodating unrestricted intercontinental operations at any hour. In Accra, Accra International Airport (ACC / DGAA) is generally operated on a 24-hour basis as well, though prior coordination with ground handlers is strongly recommended for late-night and early-morning arrivals, as staffing and customs availability may vary outside peak hours. Our operations team handles all permit applications, slot requests, and handler coordination in advance to ensure a seamless experience regardless of your chosen departure time.
How Can I Arrange Ground Transfers Between Abu Dhabi and Accra City Centres?+
In Abu Dhabi, Al Bateen Executive Airport's central location means a chauffeured luxury vehicle , typically a Mercedes S-Class or Range Rover , can reach the Emirates Palace Mandarin Oriental or the ADNEC Convention Centre in under 10 minutes. For maximum exclusivity, we can also coordinate a helicopter transfer from a dedicated helipad directly to your destination within the Abu Dhabi corniche area. On arrival in Accra, the 9 km transfer from ACC to the Cantonments or Airport Residential Area is most comfortably completed in a chauffeured luxury SUV; we work with premium ground operators to ensure a seamless ramp-to-hotel experience. For ultra-VIP access, a helicopter transfer is available from Accra to nearby destinations. Our team arranges both ends of the journey, ensuring continuity from aircraft door to final destination.
What Is the Luggage Capacity on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Accra?+
On a large-cabin jet such as the Gulfstream G450, the dedicated aft baggage compartment typically accommodates 10 to 14 standard suitcases, with additional space for cabin bags. The Bombardier Global 5000 features a comparable hold and a mid-cabin closet, making it practical for extended trips with significant luggage. If you are travelling with oversized items , sports equipment, musical instruments, exhibition materials, or diplomatic cargo , the Global 7500's walk-in baggage bay is particularly well-suited. Precise hold dimensions and payload limits vary by aircraft tail; our advisors confirm exact capacities at the time of booking and can arrange supplementary cargo flights if volumes are very large.
Can My Pets Travel with Me on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Accra?+
Absolutely , we specialise in pet-friendly private jet travel, and your four-legged family members are warmly welcome in the cabin with you. For travel from the UAE to Ghana, both countries require up-to-date health certificates, valid vaccination records (including rabies), and , in most cases , an official import permit from Ghana's Veterinary Services Directorate. Our ground team will advise you on all required documentation well in advance to ensure full regulatory compliance and a stress-free experience for both you and your companions. Cats, dogs, and other pets travel in the cabin rather than in a hold, ensuring they remain comfortable and close to their owners throughout the flight. We also coordinate with FBO handlers at both ends to facilitate any veterinary inspections that may be required on arrival.
