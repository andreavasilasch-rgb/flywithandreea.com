How Much Does It Cost to Charter a Private Jet from Algiers to Phnom Penh?+
Chartering a private jet from Algiers to Phnom Penh (AUH) typically costs between approximately $32,400 and $91,800 one-way, with a heavy jet starting from around $34,800.
