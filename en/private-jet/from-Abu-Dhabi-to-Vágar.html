The route

Abu Dhabi → Vágar.

Chartering a long-range private jet from Abu Dhabi to Vágar on the Faroe Islands is one of the most distinctive intercontinental itineraries in private aviation, linking the Gulf with one of the North Atlantic's most dramatic and remote archipelagos. Our team of aviation advisors operates around the clock to source the right aircraft for this demanding sector, which requires careful consideration of Vágar's unique approach conditions and the limited handling infrastructure on the island. Whether you are leading an executive expedition, attending a private retreat, or exploring the Faroe Islands' extraordinary landscapes, we structure the complete journey for you.

Good to know

Abu Dhabi to Vágar. Everything you need to know.

How Much Does It Cost To Charter A Private Jet Between Abu Dhabi And Vágar?+
A private jet charter from Abu Dhabi to Vágar is priced from approximately €90,000 to €165,000, reflecting the over 6,000 km routing, the limited infrastructure at the destination, and the specialised aircraft performance characteristics required. Vágar Airport's runway and approach profile are compatible with mid to large cabin jets but may restrict the very largest ultra-long range aircraft under certain conditions. Cost variables include aircraft positioning, North Atlantic routing permit requirements, fuel pricing at remote FBO locations, extended crew duty cycle management, and the comparative scarcity of return charter opportunities from the Faroe Islands that might otherwise offset empty-leg costs.
What Are The Best Airports For Private Flights Between Abu Dhabi And Vágar?+
Abu Dhabi International Airport (AUH, OMAA) offers a full-service private aviation terminal with 24-hour handling capability. Vágar Airport (FAE, EKVG) is the sole commercial and general aviation gateway to the Faroe Islands, located approximately 45 km northwest of the capital Torshavn, and serves all traffic to the archipelago. The airport sits at an elevation of 282 metres above sea level amid challenging North Atlantic weather conditions, requiring pilots with specific North Atlantic and RNAV approach qualifications. Our flight operations team verifies crew certification, aircraft performance, and weather minima for every Vágar mission to ensure safety and operational reliability.
Which Aircraft Models Are Most Frequently Chartered Between Abu Dhabi And Vágar?+
In 2025, the Gulfstream G550, Bombardier Global 6000, and Dassault Falcon 7X were the most commonly chartered aircraft for the Abu Dhabi to Faroe Islands sector, all offering the range, reliability, and operational capability required for this demanding routing. The Gulfstream G550 in particular is well regarded for its performance at challenging island airports with its proven North Atlantic operational history. A Private Aviation Advisor can assess the specific requirements of your itinerary, including the optimal fuel stop strategy if applicable and the most suitable cabin configuration for your group.
What Is The Typical Flight Duration Between Abu Dhabi And Vágar?+
A private jet flight from Abu Dhabi (AUH) to Vágar (FAE) takes approximately 8 to 10 hours depending on the routing, wind conditions at North Atlantic cruising altitudes, and whether a technical fuel stop is incorporated en route. Certain ultra-long range aircraft can achieve this sector non-stop, while others may incorporate a brief stop at a convenient hub such as London, Copenhagen, or Bergen. The private aviation terminal at Abu Dhabi allows departure within 15 to 20 minutes of arrival at the FBO, and onboard catering, connectivity, and fully reclining seating ensure that the considerable flight time passes in genuine comfort and, for business travellers, in productive working conditions.
Are There Any Night Flight Or Operational Restrictions For The Abu Dhabi To Vágar Route?+
Abu Dhabi International Airport (AUH) is a 24-hour operation with no curfew restrictions affecting private aviation. Vágar Airport (FAE, EKVG) operates with published hours generally from 06:00 to 23:30 local time, subject to seasonal and operational variation, and instrument approach procedures at Vágar are subject to specific weather minima given the terrain and oceanic atmospheric conditions surrounding the Faroe Islands. Night approaches at Vágar require certified crews with appropriate qualifications and current experience on the published approaches. Our flight operations department conducts a comprehensive pre-flight briefing on all Vágar-bound missions covering current NOTAMs, weather trends, and alternate airport planning.
How Can I Arrange Ground Transfers When Flying Private Between Abu Dhabi And Vágar?+
We organise professional ground transportation at both ends of the journey as part of your integrated travel arrangement. From Abu Dhabi, a chauffeur service collects you from your residence, the Yas Marina Circuit precinct, or any hotel in the emirate. On arrival at Vágar Airport, a dedicated vehicle transfers you across the striking island landscape to Torshavn's historic Tinganes quarter, the Atlantic Airways Hotel, or any private accommodation you have reserved across the archipelago. For clients wishing to explore multiple Faroe Islands during their stay, we can assist in arranging helicopter charters to the more remote islands in the group, including Mykines, known for its puffin colonies, and Suduroy.
What Is The Luggage Capacity When Chartering A Private Jet From Abu Dhabi To Vágar?+
Large cabin jets appropriate to this route such as the Gulfstream G550 or Bombardier Global 6000 typically accommodate between 12 and 18 standard suitcases in the dedicated cargo hold in addition to cabin bags. For expedition-style travel to the Faroe Islands, which may involve outdoor equipment, photographic gear, or specialist clothing for the North Atlantic climate, we recommend advising our team of luggage weights and dimensions at the time of booking so that the aircraft with the most appropriate hold capacity can be confirmed. Certain ultra-long range aircraft offer pressurised, climate-controlled baggage compartments that are particularly well suited to protecting sensitive equipment on long-haul sectors.
Can I Travel With My Pet On A Private Jet Between Abu Dhabi And Vágar?+
Private jet travel is the most considerate way to include your cats, dogs, or other companion animals in your journey to the Faroe Islands, with pets travelling in the cabin alongside their owners rather than in cargo. Entry of pets into the Faroe Islands from the UAE requires compliance with Faroese veterinary import regulations, which include a valid health certificate endorsed by an accredited UAE veterinary authority, documented rabies vaccination within the required timeframe, microchip identification, and a pre-arrival import permit from the Faroese Food and Veterinary Authority. Our team compiles the full documentation checklist specific to this corridor and ensures every requirement is met before departure so your beloved companions arrive as smoothly as you do.
