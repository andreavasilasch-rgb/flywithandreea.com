The route

Abu Dhabi → Osaka.

A private jet from Abu Dhabi to Osaka connects the UAE capital with Japan's most exuberant city: a metropolis of extraordinary food culture (takoyaki, okonomiyaki, and the finest ramen in Japan), the neon-lit Dotonbori entertainment district, the spectacular Osaka Castle, the ancient Buddhist temples of Shitennoji, and the perfect gateway to Kyoto (35 minutes by Shinkansen), Nara with its sacred deer and Todaiji Temple, Hiroshima and Miyajima Island, and the extraordinary Koya-san mountain monastery complex. Osaka Kansai International Airport is one of the world's most remarkable engineering achievements, built entirely on an artificial island in Osaka Bay. At 7,693 km, direct non-stop scheduled services confirm approximately 9 hours 30 minutes on this route. Our advisors manage all details 24/7.

Good to know

Abu Dhabi to Osaka. Everything you need to know.

How Much Does It Cost to Charter a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Osaka?+
Chartering a private jet from Abu Dhabi to Osaka Kansai (KIX) typically costs between approximately $95,000 and $155,000 one-way. An ultra-long-range jet such as the Gulfstream G650ER covers the 7,693 km sector with one technical stop, starting from around $98,000. Direct non-stop scheduled services confirm approximately 9h30m on this route. The Bombardier Global 7500 (range approximately 14,260 km) provides a direct non-stop service, ranging from $135,000 to $155,000+. Key pricing variables include Japan's MLIT (Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism) landing permission requirements and aircraft positioning.
Which Airports Are Used for Private Jet Flights Between Abu Dhabi and Osaka?+
From Abu Dhabi, Zayed International Airport (AUH / OMAA) accommodates larger jets; Al Bateen Executive Airport (AZI / OMAD) is the preferred private hub. In Osaka, the primary gateway is Kansai International Airport (KIX / RJBB), one of the great engineering achievements of the late 20th century: an airport built entirely on a man-made island in Osaka Bay, connected to the Osaka mainland by a 3.75 km bridge. It opened in 1994 and serves as Japan's western international gateway. Private jet handling is available through dedicated FBO services. Osaka Itami Airport (ITM / RJOO), on the mainland approximately 15 km from Osaka city centre, handles primarily domestic traffic.
Which Aircraft Models Are Recommended for Private Jet Flights from Abu Dhabi to Osaka?+
The Abu Dhabi to Osaka sector (7,693 km) requires ultra-long-range capability:

1. Gulfstream G650ER - Range approximately 12,960 km; one technical stop; sleeping suites; the most commonly chartered aircraft on this route.
2. Bombardier Global 7500 - Range approximately 14,260 km; direct non-stop; four cabin zones including a bedroom.
3. Dassault Falcon 10X - Range approximately 15,400 km; direct non-stop; highly fuel-efficient.

A dedicated Private Aviation Advisor will match the ideal aircraft.
What Is the Typical Flight Duration for a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Osaka?+
With one technical stop, the Abu Dhabi (AZI / AUH) to Osaka Kansai (KIX) total block time is approximately 12 to 13 hours. On a direct non-stop ultra-long-range jet, flight time is approximately 9 hours 30 minutes, confirmed by direct scheduled services. Osaka city centre and Dotonbori are approximately 50 minutes from Kansai by car or 75 minutes by Haruka Limited Express train; Kyoto is 35 minutes from Shin-Osaka by Shinkansen; and Nara is approximately 50 minutes.
Are There Special Operational Considerations for Osaka Kansai Airport?+
Kansai International Airport (KIX / RJBB) requires advance MLIT landing permission and slot coordination for private jet arrivals. The airport operates 24 hours a day with no curfew, making it one of the few major Japanese airports that can accommodate unrestricted operations. All international private charter flights into Japan require advance Japanese immigration and quarantine coordination. Our operations team manages all MLIT, JCAB, and customs coordination as standard.
How Can I Arrange Ground Transfers After Landing at Kansai Airport?+
We coordinate premium chauffeured transfers from Kansai's private handling area. Osaka city centre and Dotonbori are approximately 50 minutes; Universal Studios Japan 45 minutes; Kyoto and the Fushimi Inari shrine approximately 75 minutes by car or 35 minutes from Shin-Osaka by Shinkansen; and Nara 60 minutes. For guests staying at the Park Hyatt Kyoto, Aman Kyoto, or equivalent properties, we coordinate all arrival and routing logistics.
What Is the Luggage Capacity on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Osaka?+
On an ultra-long-range jet, walk-in baggage bays accommodate 14 to 20 bags. Our advisors confirm all specifications at booking.
Can My Pets Travel with Me on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Osaka?+
Japan has strict biosecurity controls. Pet imports require advance MAFF (Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries) rabies-free country certification; as the UAE is not on Japan's rabies-free list, animals arriving from the UAE are subject to mandatory quarantine of up to 180 days upon arrival. This effectively makes bringing pets directly from the UAE to Japan impractical for short visits. Our ground team can provide a full briefing on the specific documentation and quarantine requirements.
