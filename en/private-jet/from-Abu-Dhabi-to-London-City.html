The route

Abu Dhabi → London-City.

A private jet from Abu Dhabi to London City Airport (LCY) offers the most direct private aviation access to the City of London and Canary Wharf, placing arriving passengers within 10 to 15 minutes of two of the world's most important financial districts. London City is the preferred London gateway for corporate finance, investment banking, and legal professionals who value minimal transfer time above all other considerations. Its location in the Royal Docks of Newham, 11 km east of the City, means that upon landing, the Square Mile, Canary Wharf, and the West End are each accessible within 20 to 30 minutes. Important note: London City's short runway (1,500 m) and its mandatory 5.5-degree steep approach path mean that only specific certified aircraft types can operate there. Our advisors will confirm aircraft eligibility for every LCY booking.

Good to know

Abu Dhabi to London-City. Everything you need to know.

How Much Does It Cost to Charter a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to London City Airport?+
Chartering a private jet from Abu Dhabi directly to London City Airport (LCY) is an operationally specialised booking, typically costing between approximately $82,000 and $140,000 one-way. The routing typically involves a large-cabin jet to a staging London airport (Heathrow, Gatwick, or Farnborough), with a repositioning leg to LCY in a certified smaller aircraft, or a direct service in a midsize or super-midsize jet certified for LCY's steep approach. Aircraft certified for LCY include select midsize and super-midsize jets; no large-cabin widebody jets can operate at LCY. Our operations team advises the precise routing and aircraft combination for each LCY booking.
What Are the Operational Restrictions at London City Airport?+
London City Airport (LCY / EGLC) has the following key operational parameters:

1. Runway length: 1,508 m (the original 1,080 m extended in 1992), requiring all operating aircraft to have certified short-field performance.
2. Approach angle: 5.5 degrees (standard ILS approaches are 3 degrees); all aircraft and crew must be specifically certified for this steep approach.
3. Aircraft types certified include: select midsize jets, super-midsize jets (Embraer Legacy 500, Dassault Falcon 7X with steep-approach certification), and some business turboprops.
4. Large-cabin jets such as the Gulfstream G450, Global 5000, and comparable aircraft cannot operate at LCY; they can be staged at Heathrow, Gatwick, or Farnborough.
5. The airport is closed from midday Saturday to midday Sunday.
6. Slot restrictions apply; advance booking is essential.

For most long-range Gulf to London routings, our operations team recommends either Heathrow, Gatwick, Biggin Hill, or Farnborough as the arrival airport, with a road transfer to central London.
Which Aircraft Models Can Operate at London City Airport?+
Aircraft certified for LCY's steep approach and short runway include a select group:

1. Embraer Legacy 450 / 500 (Super-Midsize) - Steep-approach certified; excellent LCY performers.
2. Dassault Falcon 7X (Large-Cabin) - Steep-approach certification available; widest cabin that can operate at LCY.
3. Cessna Citation CJ series (Light Jets) - LCY-compatible for shorter-range segments.
4. Select midsize jets with appropriate steep-approach certification.

For most Abu Dhabi to London City routings, a two-leg approach is recommended: large-cabin jet to Heathrow or Farnborough, then a certified jet repositioned to LCY, or direct transfer from the arrival airport to the client's City/Canary Wharf destination by road.
What Is the Journey Duration from Abu Dhabi to London City Airport?+
The total journey time from Abu Dhabi (AUH) to a London City arrival depends on routing. A direct LCY-certified jet from Abu Dhabi (with stop) would be approximately 9 to 11 hours total block time. Via the recommended staging approach (large-cabin jet to Heathrow plus road transfer), the total journey is approximately 8 to 9 hours. The key advantage is that from LCY, the City of London is 15 to 20 minutes and Canary Wharf is 10 to 15 minutes, making the airport the fastest London option for financial district arrivals despite the routing complexity.
Are There Additional Slot and Booking Requirements for London City Airport?+
Yes. London City Airport is a slot-controlled airport; advance slot reservations are mandatory for all movements. The airport's Saturday afternoon to Sunday morning closure must be factored into scheduling. All LCY private jet bookings require advance coordination with the airport authority and FBO. Our operations team handles all slot requests, crew certifications, and ground coordination for every LCY booking as standard.
How Can I Arrange Ground Transfers After Landing at London City Airport?+
We coordinate premium chauffeured transfers directly from LCY's compact terminal, where aircraft are accessible without the queues of larger airports. The City of London and Bank is 15 minutes; Canary Wharf and the HSBC/Barclays towers 10 minutes; Mayfair and Mayfair hotels 25 minutes; and the West End 30 minutes.
What Is the Luggage Capacity for London City Airport Operations?+
Aircraft operating at LCY have more limited hold capacity than large-cabin jets. A midsize or super-midsize jet accommodates 4 to 10 bags depending on the aircraft type. For clients with substantial luggage, we recommend staging at Heathrow or Gatwick with the baggage transferred to your London destination by road while you travel in the repositioned LCY aircraft.
Can My Pets Travel with Me on a Private Jet to London City Airport?+
Yes, for pets certified to enter the UK, travel on the private jet leg is possible. UK pet entry requires microchipping, rabies vaccination, titre test, tapeworm treatment, and advance APHA documentation, with a lead time of at least 4 months. Our ground team provides a full briefing.
