How Much Does It Cost to Charter a Private Jet from Abidjan to Bilbao?+
Chartering a private jet from Abidjan to Bilbao (BIO) typically costs between approximately EUR 30,000 and EUR 85,000 one-way, with a heavy jet starting from around EUR 32,400. The main variables are positioning, handling at each end, and how busy the route is at the time of year. Operations file Bilbao under its ICAO code LEBB, paired with the IATA code BIO. Fly With Andreea is on hand to arrange it as and when you wish.
Which Airports Are Used for Private Jet Flights Between Abidjan and Bilbao?+
From Abidjan, Abidjan ABJ (DIAP) offers full international handling at the economic capital of Cote d'Ivoire, with permit coordination, approximately 15 km from the centre. In Bilbao, the gateway is Bilbao BIO (LEBB), approximately 12 km out, with full handling at the gateway to the Basque Country. Private operations are handled through dedicated FBO services, with AENA and ENAIRE handling managed as standard. When the time is right, Fly With Andreea will see to every detail.
