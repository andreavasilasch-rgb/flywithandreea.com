Good to know

Abu Dhabi to Hong Kong. Everything you need to know.

How Much Does It Cost to Charter a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Hong Kong?+
Chartering a private jet from Abu Dhabi to Hong Kong (HKG) typically costs between approximately $82,000 and $135,000 one-way. A large-cabin jet such as the Gulfstream G450 provides a comfortable non-stop service, starting from around $85,000. Ultra-long-range jets such as the Global 5000 or G650ER, with greater cabin volume, range from $115,000 to $135,000+. Key pricing variables include Hong Kong ATCA handling fees, aircraft availability, and demand around major Hong Kong financial events and the spring horse racing season.
Which Airports Are Used for Private Jet Flights Between Abu Dhabi and Hong Kong?+
From Abu Dhabi, Al Bateen Executive Airport (AZI / OMAD) is the preferred departure hub; Zayed International Airport (AUH / OMAA) accommodates larger aircraft. In Hong Kong, the gateway is Hong Kong International Airport (HKG / VHHH), located on the island of Chek Lap Kok in western Hong Kong. The world's busiest cargo airport and one of the busiest passenger airports globally, it handled nearly 61 million passengers in 2025. Named 'Chek Lap Kok' to distinguish from its predecessor Kai Tak, it opened in 1998 and is operated by the Airport Authority Hong Kong. Private aviation is handled through dedicated general-aviation facilities with full customs and immigration on site.
Which Aircraft Models Are Recommended for Private Jet Flights from Abu Dhabi to Hong Kong?+
The Abu Dhabi, Hong Kong sector (6,017 km) is best served by large-cabin jets:

1. Gulfstream G450 (Large-Cabin Jet) , Range 7,800 km; guaranteed non-stop; stand-up cabin for up to 14 passengers with full galley.
2. Bombardier Global 5000 (Ultra-Long-Range Jet) , Range 9,600 km; three-zone cabin; excellent for the 7 to 8 hour sector.
3. Gulfstream G650ER (Ultra-Long-Range Jet) , Range 12,960 km; the ultimate comfort option with maximum speed.

A dedicated Private Aviation Advisor will identify the best available aircraft.
What Is the Typical Flight Duration for a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Hong Kong?+
On a non-stop large-cabin jet, the Abu Dhabi (AZI / AUH) to Hong Kong (HKG) flight time is approximately 7 hours to 7 hours 45 minutes. On arrival at HKG, the Kowloon waterfront and Hong Kong Island CBD are accessible via the Airport Express in 24 minutes (from the arrivals hall) or approximately 40 minutes by chauffeured vehicle via the Tsing Ma Bridge. The Four Seasons Hong Kong and the Mandarin Oriental are approximately 40 minutes by road from the airport.
Are There Night Flight Restrictions at Hong Kong International Airport?+
Hong Kong International Airport (HKG / VHHH) operates 24 hours a day with no night-flight curfew, providing complete scheduling flexibility. Hong Kong ATCA and CAD (Civil Aviation Department) coordination are managed by our operations team as standard for every booking. Al Bateen in Abu Dhabi is similarly unrestricted.
How Can I Arrange Ground Transfers After Landing at Hong Kong Airport?+
We coordinate premium chauffeured transfers , Rolls-Royce Phantom or Mercedes S-Class , from the private handling area at HKG. Central Hong Kong's financial district is approximately 35 to 40 minutes via the Tsing Ma Bridge; Kowloon's Tsim Sha Tsui waterfront approximately 40 minutes. For guests staying at the Peninsula Hong Kong, Four Seasons Hong Kong, or the Rosewood, we pre-arrange full ramp-to-lobby logistics. Private speedboat transfers from the Airport Ferry Terminal to Hong Kong Island, Kowloon, or Macau can also be arranged.
What Is the Luggage Capacity on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Hong Kong?+
On a large-cabin jet, the aft hold accommodates 10 to 14 suitcases plus cabin bags. On an ultra-long-range jet, walk-in baggage bays provide even greater flexibility. Our advisors confirm all specifications at booking.
Can My Pets Travel with Me on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Hong Kong?+
Hong Kong has strict pet import regulations: dogs and cats must be microchipped, vaccinated against rabies, and hold an import licence issued by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department (AFCD) , which must be applied for in advance. UAE-origin animals require additional rabies titre testing and may be subject to a short quarantine or enhanced inspection on arrival. Our ground team provides a comprehensive advance briefing and coordinates with HKG private handlers for arrival veterinary inspections.
