Ahmedabad to Gyor. Everything you need to know.

How Much Does It Cost to Charter a Private Jet from Ahmedabad to Gyor?+
Chartering a private jet from Ahmedabad to Gyor (QGY) typically costs between approximately EUR 52,000 and EUR 115,000 one-way, with a large-cabin jet starting from around EUR 56,160. The main variables are positioning, handling at each end, and how busy the route is at the time of year. Fly With Andreea is on hand to arrange it as and when you wish.
Which Airports Are Used for Private Jet Flights Between Ahmedabad and Gyor?+
From Ahmedabad, Ahmedabad AMD (VAAH) offers full international handling at the gateway to Gujarat, approximately 10 km from the centre. In Gyor, the gateway is Gyor QGY (LHPR), approximately 12 km out, with general aviation handling between Vienna and Budapest. Private operations are handled through dedicated FBO services, with HungaroControl handling managed as standard. Filed as LHPR in the flight plan, Gyor QGY reads the same to handlers worldwide. Our advisors at Fly With Andreea are glad to handle the details whenever you are ready.
Which Aircraft Models Are Recommended for Private Jet Flights from Ahmedabad to Gyor?+
The Ahmedabad to Gyor sector (approximately 5,550 km) is well served by the following:

2. the Falcon 2000 (heavy jet) with a range of approximately 5,500 km.
3. the Falcon 10X (ultra-long-range jet) with a range of approximately 13,900 km.
A dedicated Private Aviation Advisor will match the ideal aircraft to your party and schedule.
What Is the Typical Flight Duration for a Private Jet from Ahmedabad to Gyor?+
The Ahmedabad (AMD) to Gyor (QGY) block time is approximately 7 hours 7 minutes to 8 hours 23 minutes. On arrival, Gyor is approximately 15 minutes from the airport; Pannonhalma approximately 30 minutes. Whenever it suits you, the Fly With Andreea team can take care of the arrangements.

Gyor QGY (LHPR) is a regional field where out of hours operations are requested ahead of time. Late movements are coordinated in advance. Our operations team manages all HungaroControl handling as standard. The Fly With Andreea team is happy to shape this around your plans.

We coordinate premium chauffeured transfers from the airport. Gyor is approximately 15 minutes from the airport; Pannonhalma approximately 30 minutes. What Is the Luggage Capacity on a Private Jet from Ahmedabad to Gyor?+
On a large-cabin jet, the hold accommodates ten to fourteen suitcases plus cabin bags in the aft hold. Our advisors confirm exact specifications at booking.
