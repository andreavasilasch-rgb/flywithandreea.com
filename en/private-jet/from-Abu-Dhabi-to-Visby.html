The route

Abu Dhabi → Visby.

Chartering a large cabin or ultra-long range jet from Abu Dhabi to Visby connects the capital of the UAE with the medieval UNESCO World Heritage island of Gotland in the Baltic Sea. Our aviation experts are available 24/7 to source the aircraft that best matches your travel requirements, whether you are flying for leisure or on a private retreat. With the nearest major Swedish airport requiring an onward transfer to the island, we coordinate the full journey end to end for a completely seamless experience.

Good to know

Abu Dhabi to Visby. Everything you need to know.

How Much Does It Cost To Charter A Private Jet Between Abu Dhabi And Visby?+
Chartering a private jet from Abu Dhabi (AUH) to Visby (VBY) typically costs from approximately €95,000 to €175,000, depending on the aircraft category selected and the operational specifics of the mission. This intercontinental route covering over 5,200 km requires a large cabin or ultra-long range jet capable of non-stop or single-stop performance. Pricing variables include seasonal demand across Scandinavian summer periods, aircraft repositioning from the originating base, fuel surcharges applicable to long-haul sectors, crew rest requirements on extended duty cycles, and any additional landing or overflight permit fees across the multiple airspaces transited.
What Are The Best Airports For Private Flights Between Abu Dhabi And Visby?+
Abu Dhabi International Airport (AUH, OMAA) serves as the primary departure point on the UAE side, offering a dedicated private aviation terminal with full FBO services, VIP lounges, and 24-hour handling. On the Swedish side, Visby Airport (VBY, ESSV) is a regional facility located approximately 3 km north of Visby city centre on the island of Gotland and is accessible by general aviation and smaller charter aircraft. For larger ultra-long range jets that cannot operate into Visby directly, Stockholm Arlanda Airport (ARN, ESSA) or Stockholm Bromma Airport (BMA, ESSB) serve as the nearest major alternatives on the Swedish mainland, from which a connecting flight, helicopter, or high-speed ferry can complete the island transfer. Our advisors assess the aircraft performance requirements and select the optimal routing for each itinerary.
Which Aircraft Models Are Most Frequently Chartered Between Abu Dhabi And Visby?+
In 2025, the Gulfstream G550, Bombardier Global 6000, and Dassault Falcon 7X were among the most frequently chartered aircraft for the Abu Dhabi to northern Europe sector, offering the range, cabin comfort, and reliability required for missions exceeding 5,000 km. For clients requiring non-stop ultra-long range performance with a wider cabin, the Gulfstream G650ER and Bombardier Global 7500 represent the premium choice on this route. A dedicated Private Aviation Advisor can assess your specific passenger count, luggage requirements, and scheduling needs to recommend the most appropriate aircraft.
What Is The Typical Flight Duration Between Abu Dhabi And Visby?+
A direct private jet flight from Abu Dhabi (AUH) to Stockholm Arlanda (ARN) takes approximately 5.1 to 6 hours depending on aircraft type, wind conditions, and chosen routing. A connecting short sector from Stockholm to Visby Airport (VBY) adds approximately 5.1 to 6 hours. One of the defining advantages of private aviation on intercontinental routes is the ability to arrive at the FBO terminal just 15 to 20 minutes before departure, bypassing commercial check-in queues entirely. Catering, connectivity, and full flat-bed configurations on large cabin jets ensure maximum productivity or rest throughout the journey.
Are There Any Night Flight Or Operational Restrictions For The Abu Dhabi To Visby Route?+
Abu Dhabi International Airport (AUH) operates on a 24-hour basis with no scheduled night curfew for private aviation, providing full scheduling flexibility at the origin. Visby Airport (VBY) is a regional facility with published operating hours generally between 06:00 and 22:00 local time, with specific slot availability subject to seasonal variation and advance notice requirements. Operations outside standard hours at Visby typically require prior coordination with Swedish air traffic authorities. For time-sensitive late-night or early-morning arrivals on Gotland, our advisors may propose routing through Stockholm Bromma (BMA, ESSB) or Arlanda (ARN, ESSA), which offer extended or 24-hour handling capability, followed by a surface or air connection to the island.
How Can I Arrange Ground Transfers When Flying Private Between Abu Dhabi And Visby?+
We arrange luxury ground transportation at both ends of the journey as a fully integrated part of your itinerary. Departing from Abu Dhabi, a chauffeur-driven vehicle can collect you from your residence, Saadiyat Island retreat, or the Emirates Palace precinct and deliver you directly to the private aviation terminal. On arrival at Visby, a pre-arranged vehicle will be waiting to transfer you to the medieval walled city, the Grand Hotel Gutekalla, or the historic waterfront. For clients arriving during summer when Gotland hosts the Almedalen political week or major cultural festivals, advance ground logistics planning is strongly recommended to navigate limited island transport availability.
What Is The Luggage Capacity When Chartering A Private Jet From Abu Dhabi To Visby?+
Large cabin jets such as the Gulfstream G550 or Bombardier Global 6000, which are the appropriate aircraft categories for this intercontinental distance, typically accommodate between 14 and 20 standard suitcases in addition to carry-on luggage, with spacious dedicated cargo holds separate from the passenger cabin. For clients travelling with sporting equipment, sailing gear appropriate to Gotland's regatta season, or significant luggage volumes, the Bombardier Global 7500 offers one of the largest cargo capacities in the ultra-long range category. If your group exceeds 10 passengers or your luggage requirements are unusually heavy, our advisors can evaluate freighter-supplemented solutions or split luggage logistics.
Can I Travel With My Pet On A Private Jet Between Abu Dhabi And Visby?+
Private jet charter is the most comfortable and dignified way to travel with your cats, dogs, or other companions, who are welcome in the cabin with you rather than being separated in cargo holds. For the Abu Dhabi to Sweden corridor, Swedish customs regulations require valid EU pet passports or third-country health certificates, proof of rabies vaccination, and microchip identification compliant with ISO 11784 standards. UAE exit requirements for pets must also be confirmed in advance. Our team coordinates all documentation requirements well ahead of departure so that your four-legged family members pass through both UAE and Swedish border controls without complications, arriving on Gotland as relaxed as you are.
