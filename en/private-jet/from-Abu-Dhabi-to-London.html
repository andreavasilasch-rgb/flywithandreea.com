Good to know

Abu Dhabi to London. Everything you need to know.

How Much Does It Cost to Charter a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to London?+
Chartering a private jet from Abu Dhabi to London typically costs between approximately $72,000 and $130,000 one-way, depending on aircraft category and the specific London airport selected. A large-cabin jet such as the Gulfstream G450 provides a direct non-stop service to Heathrow (LHR), Gatwick (LGW), Stansted (STN), or Biggin Hill (BQH), starting from around $75,000. For the central city convenience of London City Airport (LCY), only smaller jets certified for its steep approach and short runway can operate there; positioning from a larger aircraft to LCY adds cost. Our advisors design the optimal routing for each client's requirements.
Which London Airports Are Available for Private Jet Arrivals?+
London is served by five main airports for private jet operations:

1. Heathrow (LHR / EGLL) - 23 km west; world's 4th busiest airport; full private handling; night curfew applies.
2. Gatwick (LGW / EGKK) - 47 km south; one of Europe's busiest airports; private handling available; some night restrictions.
3. London City (LCY / EGLC) - 11 km east of the City; only smaller jets certified for 1,500 m runway and 5.5-degree steep approach can operate here; ideal for Canary Wharf/City access.
4. Stansted (STN / EGSS) - 56 km north-east; busy private aviation hub; full 24-hour operations available.
5. Biggin Hill (BQH / EGKB) - 26 km south-east; dedicated private jet airport; excellent FBO facilities.

Farnborough (FAB) is also available as a premium private aviation hub, 50 km south-west.

Our operations team advises on the optimal London gateway for each client.
Which Aircraft Models Are Recommended for Private Jet Flights from Abu Dhabi to London?+
The Abu Dhabi to London sector (approximately 5,500 km) is ideal for large-cabin jets:

1. Gulfstream G450 (Large-Cabin Jet) - Range approximately 7,800 km; non-stop to Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, or Biggin Hill; stand-up cabin for up to 14 passengers.
2. Challenger 350 (Super-Midsize Jet) - Range approximately 5,900 km; may require a brief refuelling stop; flat-floor cabin.
3. Bombardier Global 5000 (Ultra-Long-Range Jet) - Non-stop; three-zone cabin; superior comfort for the 7-hour sector.

For London City (LCY): only mid-size and smaller jets with steep approach certification can operate; contact us for specific aircraft options.
What Is the Typical Flight Duration for a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to London?+
The non-stop flight time from Abu Dhabi (AZI / AUH) to London (LHR / LGW / STN / BQH / LCY) is approximately 6 hours 45 minutes to 7 hours 30 minutes on a large-cabin jet. Journey time to your London destination varies significantly by arrival airport: from Heathrow, central London is 40 to 60 minutes by car; from Biggin Hill, 30 to 40 minutes to central and south London; from London City, 15 to 20 minutes to Canary Wharf and the City.
Are There Night Flight Restrictions at London Airports?+
Heathrow (LHR) operates a strict night curfew from approximately 23:00 to 06:00; Gatwick (LGW) has similar restrictions. Stansted (STN) has more flexible night operating hours. Biggin Hill (BQH) and Farnborough (FAB) have their own operating schedules. London City (LCY) is closed between midday Saturday and midday Sunday. Our operations team advises on the optimal airport for each timing requirement.
How Can I Arrange Ground Transfers After Landing in London?+
We coordinate premium chauffeured transfers from any London airport, including Rolls-Royce, Bentley, or Mercedes S-Class. From Biggin Hill, central London is 35 minutes; from Heathrow, 40 to 60 minutes; from Stansted, 45 to 75 minutes depending on traffic. For guests requiring helicopter transfers between airports or to a London heliport, we coordinate all arrangements.
What Is the Luggage Capacity on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to London?+
On a large-cabin jet, the aft hold accommodates 10 to 14 suitcases plus cabin bags. Our advisors confirm all specifications at booking.
Can My Pets Travel with Me on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to London?+
Yes, your cats and dogs travel in the cabin. UK pet entry requires microchipping, rabies vaccination, a blood titre test, tapeworm treatment for dogs, and an animal health certificate, with a lead time of at least 4 months from the UAE. Our ground team provides a comprehensive advance briefing.
