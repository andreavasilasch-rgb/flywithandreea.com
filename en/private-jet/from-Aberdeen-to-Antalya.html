How much does it cost to charter a private jet from Aberdeen to Antalya?+
Charter pricing on the Aberdeen–Antalya route typically ranges from €17,000 to €38,000 for midsize and large-cabin jets. The 3,100 km sector is squarely within super-midsize range, though many leisure clients prefer large-cabin aircraft for the four-hour journey. Pricing variables include aircraft category and cabin configuration, seasonal demand peaks (July and August when Turkish Riviera demand peaks across private aviation), aircraft positioning costs from Scotland, Turkish DGCA overflight and landing permit fees, handling rates at Antalya Airport's FBO terminal, in-flight catering upgrades for the four-hour sector, and crew overnight requirements for extended Turkish stays. What airports serve private jet flights between Aberdeen and Antalya?+
Aberdeen Airport (ABZ / EGPD) handles UK departures with a general aviation terminal, private customs, and efficient airside access. Antalya Airport (AYT / LTAI) is Turkey's second-busiest airport and handles significant private jet traffic through its dedicated general aviation terminal, with FBO operators providing private lounges, meet-and-greet services, and customs facilitation. AYT sits 13 km east of Antalya city and provides direct access to the Belek golf hotel strip (20 km), Kalkan (175 km), and Bodrum (330 km) for clients connecting further west. Gazipaşa-Alanya Airport (GZP / LTFG) is a growing alternative for clients targeting the Alanya resort area, 85 km east. Fly with Andreea's advisors will recommend the optimal airport for your specific Turkish Riviera destination.
Which private jets are most frequently chartered between Aberdeen and Antalya?+
The 3,100 km Aberdeen–Antalya sector is served primarily by super-midsize and large-cabin jets in 2025. The Bombardier Challenger 350 is the most consistent choice, with its 6,300 km range, stand-up cabin, and flat-bed seating options for up to ten passengers on a four-hour Mediterranean flight. The Dassault Falcon 2000LXS offers a refined three-zone cabin, 7,400 km range, and strong Turkish airfield performance, making it popular with clients also visiting Bodrum or Dalaman. For leisure parties of ten or more, the Gulfstream G450 provides a spacious cabin with full kitchen facilities and 6,750 km range. What is the typical private jet flight time from Aberdeen to Antalya?+
Aberdeen to Antalya covers approximately 3,100 km, translating to a block time of three hours thirty minutes to four hours fifteen minutes for super-midsize and large-cabin jets routing via France, Italy, and the Balkans into Turkish airspace. Passengers using Aberdeen's private FBO arrive 15 to 20 minutes before departure, bypassing commercial check-in, security queues, and hub layovers. The FBO provides private lounges, freshly prepared catering to any specification, shower facilities, and direct airside vehicle transfers. At Antalya, the general aviation terminal processes passengers through Turkish immigration and customs within minutes, with vehicles waiting at the aircraft steps for transfer to your resort, marina, or villa.
Are there night curfews or permit requirements for private flights from Aberdeen to Antalya?+
Antalya Airport (AYT) operates 24 hours a day with no published general aviation night curfew, making it flexible for evening arrivals and early-morning departures aligned with Turkish Riviera summer itineraries. However, all foreign-registered private aircraft operating into Turkey require a prior permission (PPR) clearance from the Turkish Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA), with overflight permits for each airspace transited filed in advance. Typical Turkish PPR processing takes 24 to 48 hours. Aberdeen Airport (ABZ) has no night curfew for business aviation. Fly with Andreea's operations team initiates all Turkish permit filings, manages the approval process in real time, and holds alternative routing options to protect your departure schedule.
How do I arrange a luxury transfer from Antalya Airport to the Belek golf resorts, Kalkan, or a superyacht in Kemer Marina?+
Upon landing at Antalya Airport (AYT), Fly with Andreea coordinates luxury transfers across the Turkish Riviera. The old town Kaleiçi district of Antalya is 15 minutes by chauffeur vehicle. Kemer Marina, a popular superyacht anchorage, is 35 minutes west. For clients heading to Kalkan's exclusive villa enclave, the drive takes approximately 3 hours along the D400 coastal road; alternatively, Fly with Andreea arranges helicopter transfers from Antalya to Dalaman or Kaş helipad in under one hour. All transfers are confirmed and waiting at the general aviation apron before your aircraft lands.
What is the luggage capacity for a private jet on the Aberdeen to Antalya route?+
Super-midsize and large-cabin jets on this route Challenger 350, Falcon 2000LXS, Gulfstream G450 offer hold volumes of 900 to 1,400 litres, accommodating ten to eighteen full-size suitcases plus cabin storage, comfortably serving groups of six to twelve passengers with summer wardrobes, golf bags for Belek's championship courses, sailing kit, or dive equipment. There are no commercial-style baggage fees, no oversize penalties, and no third-party baggage handling. For clients arriving with specialist items photography equipment, medical devices, or duty-free goods subject to Turkish customs declaration your Fly with Andreea advisor will brief you on applicable import regulations in advance. Your luggage is loaded and unloaded exclusively by the operating crew.
Can my dog or cat fly with me on a private jet from Aberdeen to Antalya?+
Your beloved furry companions are welcome in the cabin alongside you on this route to the Turkish Riviera. Turkey requires the following documentation for pet imports: a health certificate issued by a UK Official Veterinarian (OV) within 10 days of travel; proof of microchipping conforming to ISO 11784/11785; a current rabies vaccination administered at least 30 days before travel; and for dogs, an FAVN rabies titre test result meeting Turkish Ministry of Agriculture standards. Certain dog breeds are restricted under Turkish law. Antalya Airport's general aviation terminal has dedicated channels for pet-import processing on private flights. Fly with Andreea's advisors will provide a breed-specific documentation checklist and connect you with OV-certified practices in your area. We recommend initiating paperwork at least 72 hours before your planned departure.
