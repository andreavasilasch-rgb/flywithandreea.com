The route

Abu Dhabi → Ankara.

A private jet from Abu Dhabi to Ankara is the most efficient and discreet way to connect the UAE capital with Turkey's seat of government, diplomatic missions, and strategic business environment. At approximately 2,600 km, this sector is comfortably served by a midsize or super-midsize jet in under 3.5 hours, making it ideal for same-day executive travel, diplomatic visits, and business delegations. Our aviation advisors are available 24/7 to source your aircraft, coordinate Turkish overflight and landing permits, and manage all ground logistics across both capitals.

Good to know

Abu Dhabi to Ankara. Everything you need to know.

How Much Does It Cost to Charter a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Ankara?+
Chartering a private jet from Abu Dhabi to Ankara (ESB) typically costs between approximately $28,000 and $58,000 one-way. A midsize jet such as the Citation XLS+ or Hawker 900XP covers the 2,600 km sector direct and starts from around $28,000. A super-midsize jet such as the Challenger 350 or large-cabin jet such as the Gulfstream G450, offering greater passenger capacity and premium cabin comfort, ranges from $40,000 to $58,000. Key pricing variables include Turkish airspace overfly and landing permit fees (required for all charter operations into Turkey), aircraft positioning, and seasonal government and diplomatic travel demand.
Which Airports Are Used for Private Jet Flights Between Abu Dhabi and Ankara?+
From Abu Dhabi, Al Bateen Executive Airport (AZI / OMAD) is the preferred departure hub, with Zayed International Airport (AUH / OMAA) available for larger aircraft. In Ankara, the primary and sole international gateway is Ankara Esenboğa Airport (ESB / LTAC), located approximately 28 km north-east of the city centre. Operated by TAV Airports Holding, Esenboğa handles nearly 14 million passengers annually and features two runways (3,398 m and 3,750 m), dedicated general-aviation terminal facilities opened in 2012, and full customs, immigration, and VIP handling services. For private aviation, dedicated FBO-style services are available through TAV-affiliated handlers, providing ramp-side vehicle access and expedited clearance for diplomatic and executive charter arrivals.
Which Aircraft Models Are Recommended for Private Jet Flights from Abu Dhabi to Ankara?+
The Abu Dhabi, Ankara sector (2,600 km) is ideally suited to midsize and super-midsize jets:

1. Citation XLS+ (Midsize Jet) , Range 3,600 km; covers this route direct with up to 9 passengers; reliable, widely available, and cost-effective.
2. Challenger 350 (Super-Midsize Jet) , Range 5,900 km; flat-floor cabin for up to 10 passengers; exceptional comfort for a government or executive delegation visit.
3. Gulfstream G450 (Large-Cabin Jet) , Range 7,800 km; stand-up cabin for up to 14; the premium choice for larger delegations or those requiring a full onboard working environment.

A dedicated Private Aviation Advisor will match the ideal aircraft to your group and schedule.
What Is the Typical Flight Duration for a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Ankara?+
The direct flight time between Abu Dhabi (AZI / AUH) and Ankara Esenboğa (ESB) is approximately 3 hours to 3 hours 30 minutes on a midsize or super-midsize jet, subject to routing and wind conditions. Departing from Al Bateen Executive Airport, passengers arrive at the FBO just 20 minutes before departure. Esenboğa Airport's dedicated general-aviation terminal ensures expedited clearance, with passengers typically in a vehicle within 20 minutes of landing , and the Grand National Assembly, key ministries, and central Kızılay district reachable within 35 to 40 minutes by road.
Are There Night Flight Restrictions at Ankara Esenboğa Airport?+
Ankara Esenboğa Airport (ESB / LTAC) operates 24 hours a day with no night-flight curfew, making it operationally flexible for early-morning or late-night diplomatic and executive arrivals. Al Bateen Executive Airport in Abu Dhabi is similarly unrestricted. All charter flights into Turkey require a valid Turkish DGCA overflight and landing permit, applied for well in advance; our operations team manages these filings as standard, ensuring clearance is in place ahead of every departure.
How Can I Arrange Ground Transfers After Landing at Ankara Esenboğa Airport?+
We coordinate premium chauffeured transfers , Mercedes S-Class saloons, V-Class vans for delegations, or armoured vehicles on request , directly from the aircraft steps at Esenboğa. The 28 km transfer to central Ankara takes approximately 30 to 40 minutes depending on traffic; we can also arrange police or escort coordination for diplomatic visits when required. For guests staying at the Sheraton Ankara, Ankara Hilton, or attending meetings at the Presidential Complex (Cumhurbaşkanlığı Külliyesi), we pre-arrange precise ramp-to-meeting timings as part of our full executive service.
What Is the Luggage Capacity on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Ankara?+
On a midsize jet such as the Citation XLS+, the aft baggage hold typically accommodates 6 to 8 standard suitcases plus cabin bags , sufficient for a business delegation or short government visit. On a super-midsize jet such as the Challenger 350, hold capacity increases to 8 to 12 bags, with a flat-floor cabin also accommodating document cases, diplomatic bags, and presentation materials. Our advisors confirm all specifications at booking.
Can My Pets Travel with Me on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Ankara?+
Yes , your beloved cats and dogs travel in the cabin with you throughout the flight. For travel from the UAE into Turkey, pets must be accompanied by a valid health certificate issued within 10 days of departure, proof of rabies vaccination, and must be microchipped. Additional import documentation may be required depending on species and the specific nature of the visit. Our ground coordination team provides a full briefing on current Turkish Ministry of Agriculture requirements and liaisons with Esenboğa handlers to facilitate any veterinary inspections on arrival.
