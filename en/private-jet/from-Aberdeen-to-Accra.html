The route

Aberdeen → Accra.

The Aberdeen–Accra corridor is a route of genuine strategic importance, connecting Scotland's oil and gas capital with Ghana's thriving commercial capital and sub-Saharan Africa's most stable investment gateway. At approximately 7,200 km, this long-haul sector demands an ultra-long-range business jet capable of delivering a productive, comfortable journey in eight to ten hours. Fly with Andreea arranges private jet charters on this route for energy executives, investors, diplomatic delegations, and high-net-worth families with ties to West Africa. Our team is available 24/7 and manages every detail from aircraft selection and catering to ground logistics, customs coordination, and overflight permits across multiple African airspaces.

Good to know

Aberdeen to Accra. Everything you need to know.

How much does it cost to charter a private jet from Aberdeen to Accra?+
Chartering a private jet between Aberdeen (ABZ) and Accra (ACC) typically costs between €45,000 and €110,000, reflecting the ultra-long-range aircraft required for the approximately 7,200 km sector and the operational complexity of routing through multiple African airspace jurisdictions. Pricing variables include aircraft type and cabin configuration, seasonal demand aligned with Ghana's investment conference calendar and peak festive-season travel (December), overflight permit fees for airspaces transited including France, Spain, Morocco, and West African states, crew rest requirements for extended duty periods, and specialist ground handling at Kotoka International Airport. Contact a Fly with Andreea advisor for a fully itemised, transparent quote.
What are the best airports for a private jet flight between Aberdeen and Accra?+
Aberdeen Airport (ABZ / EGPD) is the standard departure point for Scotland-based travellers, with full general aviation handling, private customs, and minimal taxi time between the FBO and the runway. Kotoka International Airport (ACC / DGAA) in Accra is Ghana's primary international aviation gateway and handles private jet movements through dedicated general aviation handling agents including Aviance Ghana and TAG Aviation Africa, both offering executive lounges, private customs clearance, and secure ramp access. For clients based in the north-east of Scotland needing greater slot flexibility, Edinburgh Airport (EDI / EGPH) and Glasgow International (GLA / EGPF) are practical alternatives for positioning legs. Your Fly with Andreea advisor will identify the optimal routing and handling combination for your specific departure timeline.
Which aircraft are most frequently chartered for the Aberdeen to Accra route?+
The Aberdeen–Accra distance of approximately 7,200 km places this firmly in ultra-long-range territory, where three aircraft dominate 2025 charter demand. The Gulfstream G650ER, with its 13,890 km range and best-in-class cruise speed of Mach 0.90, is the most operationally efficient choice for non-stop operation. The Bombardier Global 7500 offers a superior cabin experience four living spaces, a full-size kitchen, and a private suite for travellers who prioritise comfort over marginal cost differences. The Dassault Falcon 8X provides a tri-engine safety profile that is particularly valued on West African routes and offers 11,945 km range with a highly customisable cabin. A Fly with Andreea Private Aviation Advisor will match aircraft to your group size and onboard requirements.
What is the typical private jet flight time between Aberdeen and Accra?+
Aberdeen to Accra covers approximately 7,200 km, translating to a block time of eight to ten hours for ultra-long-range jets operating non-stop. Routing typically takes aircraft south over the UK, across France and Spain, over Morocco and Mauritania, and south-east into Ghana a great-circle path that crosses five to six FIR boundaries. Passengers using Aberdeen's private FBO can arrive just 15 to 20 minutes before departure, with no public security queues or shared terminals. The FBO provides dedicated lounges, on-demand catering, business workstations, and direct airside vehicle access. In Accra, handling agents at Kotoka are briefed ahead of arrival to ensure customs clearance and ground transport are ready at the aircraft steps.
Are there night flight curfews or airspace restrictions on the Aberdeen to Accra route?+
Aberdeen Airport (ABZ) has no published night curfew for general aviation operations, though extended-hours movements are subject to handling-agent availability and ATC staffing. Kotoka International Airport (ACC) in Accra operates 24 hours a day. However, private jet operations into Ghana require a landing permit issued by the Ghana Civil Aviation Authority (GCAA), which must be secured before departure along with overflight permits for each airspace transited including those of Morocco, Mauritania, Senegal, and Guinea all of which carry varying lead times and fees. Fly with Andreea's operations team initiates all permit applications well in advance, monitors approval status in real time, and holds contingency routing options should any airspace become restricted. Your departure will not be delayed by paperwork.
How do I arrange a luxury transfer from Accra airport to the city or the Labadi Beach area?+
Fly with Andreea's ground concierge team has established relationships with Accra's premium chauffeur services, coordinating fleet vehicles including Mercedes-Benz S-Class and GLS-class SUVs for the 20 to 30-minute drive from Kotoka International to the Airport Residential Area, Cantonments, or the Labadi Beach Hotel on the Atlantic coast. For clients arriving for corporate events or summits in the central business district, transfers to the Accra International Conference Centre take approximately 15 minutes by private vehicle. We also coordinate helicopter charters from Accra's Kotoka apron to coastal destinations or private estates further afield. All vehicles are confirmed on the apron before your aircraft doors open.
What is the luggage capacity for a private jet on the Aberdeen to Accra route?+
Ultra-long-range aircraft on this route Gulfstream G650ER, Global 7500, and Falcon 8X offer pressurised hold capacities ranging from approximately 1,000 to 1,600 litres, accommodating 14 to 20 full-size suitcases and extensive cabin storage for personal items. If you are travelling with specialist equipment for Ghanaian field operations survey instruments, medical supplies, presentation equipment, or ceremonial luggage for formal events advise your Fly with Andreea coordinator in advance so the correct aircraft with a suitably sized hold is selected. There are no weight penalties, no oversize-baggage fees, and no third-party baggage-handling risks. Your belongings travel in the same aircraft as you, under full custody throughout.
Can I bring my cat or dog on a private jet from Aberdeen to Accra?+
Your beloved companions are welcome aboard throughout the Aberdeen–Accra journey they travel in the cabin with you, not in the hold. Ghana requires specific documentation for importing domestic pets: a veterinary health certificate issued within 10 days of travel and endorsed by the UK's Animal and Plant Health Agency (APHA); proof of valid rabies vaccination administered at least 30 days and not more than 12 months before arrival; a microchip certificate conforming to ISO 11784/11785; and an import permit from the Veterinary Services Directorate of Ghana's Ministry of Food and Agriculture. Requirements for cats and dogs differ slightly, and restrictions on certain dog breeds apply under Ghanaian law. Fly with Andreea's advisory team will brief you on every requirement specific to your pet and connect you with APHA-certified veterinary practices. Begin the process at least 72 hours before departure for a stress-free journey.
