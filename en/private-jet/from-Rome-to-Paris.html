Good to know

Rome to Paris. Everything you need to know.

How Much Does It Cost To Charter A Private Jet Between Rome And Paris?+
Chartering a private jet between Rome and Paris typically costs between €7,000 and €20,000, depending on the aircraft category, time of year, and specific routing requirements. Pricing is shaped by variables including peak-season demand surcharges, empty-leg repositioning costs, extended ground time fees, premium catering upgrades, and any international overflight permit charges. Our advisors provide transparent all-inclusive quotes with no hidden fees, so you always know exactly what you are investing in your experience.
What Are The Best Airports For Private Flights Between Rome And Paris?+
For departure, we recommend Rome Ciampino Airport (CIA) the dedicated private jet hub for the Italian capital, 15 km from the city centre and Rome Fiumicino (FCO) for larger wide-body aircraft, ensuring smooth, swift processing for private clients. On arrival, Paris Le Bourget (LBG) Europe's busiest private aviation hub with seven FBOs and round-the-clock service and Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG) for transatlantic services and larger aircraft types provides the ideal gateway. Both facilities offer fully staffed FBOs, private customs clearance, secure onsite parking, and premium lounges, ensuring that from the moment you step off the aircraft in Paris, your journey proceeds with maximum efficiency.
Which Aircraft Models Are Most Frequently Chartered Between Rome And Paris?+
The most frequently requested aircraft on the Rome–Paris route include the Challenger 350, Hawker 900XP, and Citation XLS+. Each model is selected to match the route's specific range and cabin comfort requirements, ensuring you arrive refreshed whether you are travelling for a board meeting or a leisure escape. A dedicated Fly with Andreea Private Aviation Advisor can help you select the ideal configuration for your group size, luggage requirements, and preferred onboard amenities. Contact one of our local offices for a tailored recommendation.
What is the typical flight duration between Rome and Paris?+
A private jet between Rome and Paris typically flies approximately 2.5 to 4 hours, depending on aircraft type, routing, and prevailing winds. Unlike commercial travel, private aviation allows you to arrive at the FBO just 15–20 minutes before departure, dramatically reducing your total door-to-door travel time. On this route, the time savings compared to commercial alternatives including check-in queues, security, and connection transfers can amount to several hours.
Are there any operational or airspace considerations for the Rome–Paris route?+
Rome Ciampino (CIA) imposes night-flight restrictions between 11:00 PM and 6:00 AM for noise-sensitive aircraft categories. Our advisors can route your flight through Fiumicino (FCO) for late-night departures, or arrange a pre-positioned ground transfer to minimise any scheduling impact. Private jet passengers on the Rome–Paris route benefit from streamlined customs processing at both ends of the journey. Our operations team handles all required flight plan submissions, overflight permits, and passenger documentation well in advance of departure. If you hold a diplomatic passport or require an enhanced privacy protocol, please inform your aviation advisor so appropriate arrangements can be made at both ends of the journey.
Can I arrange a ground transfer or onward connection from Paris after landing?+
Absolutely. Upon landing, our ground concierge team can arrange a helicopter transfer from Le Bourget to the Issy-les-Moulineaux Heliport, placing you near the Champs-Élysées in under 15 minutes. We work with premium chauffeur services and helicopter operators to ensure that your onward journey begins the moment you step off the aircraft. For clients arriving in Paris for special events yacht departures, corporate summits, or cultural galas we can coordinate seamless multi-modal transfers tailored to your schedule.
What is the luggage capacity for a private jet on the Rome–Paris route?+
The aircraft most frequently used on this route comfortably accommodate 6 to 10 pieces of standard luggage, plus carry-on items. If you are travelling with specialist equipment sports gear, musical instruments, medical devices, or oversized business materials please notify your advisor in advance so we can assign an aircraft with an appropriately sized baggage hold or cargo door. Unlike commercial airlines, private jets impose no arbitrary bag fees, and our team handles all loading with discretion and care.
Can I bring my pet on a private jet between Rome and Paris?+
Yes Fly with Andreea specialises in pet-friendly private jet charters. Your companion can travel in the cabin alongside you, provided that all required health documentation, microchipping records, and import/export certificates for both Italy and France are in order. Our advisors will guide you through the precise entry requirements and connect you with approved veterinary documentation services if needed. We recommend preparing paperwork at least 72 hours before departure to ensure a fully stress-free journey for both you and your four-legged companion.
