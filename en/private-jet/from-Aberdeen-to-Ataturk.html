What airports are used for private jet flights between Aberdeen and Istanbul?+
Aberdeen Airport (ABZ / EGPD) handles UK departures with a general aviation facility and private customs. Istanbul Airport (IST / LTFM) which replaced Atatürk Airport (ISL / LTBA) for commercial operations in 2019 now handles private jet traffic through its dedicated business aviation terminal on the European side of the Bosphorus, 35 km north-west of the city centre. Atatürk Airport (LTBA) is now primarily used for general aviation and cargo operations and remains an option for private charter arrivals with prior coordination. Sabiha Gökçen International Airport (SAW / LTFJ) on the Asian side of the city is a third alternative, preferred for clients with business in Istanbul's Anatolian districts. Are there permit requirements or curfews affecting private jets on the Aberdeen to Istanbul route?+
Istanbul Airport (IST) is a 24-hour international airport with no published night curfew for general aviation operations. However, all foreign-registered private aircraft entering Turkish airspace require prior permission (PPR) from Turkey's DGCA, with overflight permits for each airspace transited filed in advance typical processing time is 24 to 48 hours. Atatürk Airport (LTBA) operates under different handling arrangements and may require separate coordination. Aberdeen Airport (ABZ) has no night curfew for business aviation. Fly with Andreea's operations team manages all Turkish regulatory requirements, coordinates with ground handlers at the correct Istanbul terminal, and monitors approval status in real time to protect your departure schedule.

Can my dog or cat travel with me on a private jet from Aberdeen to Istanbul?+
Your beloved companions are welcome in the cabin with you on this route. Turkey's pet import requirements are specific: a health certificate issued by a UK Official Veterinarian (OV) within 10 days of travel, endorsed for Turkish entry; proof of microchipping to ISO 11784/11785; a valid rabies vaccination administered at least 30 days before travel; and for dogs, a rabies antibody titre test (FAVN) meeting Turkish Ministry of Agriculture standards. Certain dog breeds are restricted under Turkish law. Istanbul Airport has designated animal reception facilities for privately arrived pets.
