The route

Abu Dhabi → Chania.

A private jet from Abu Dhabi to Chania is the ultimate way to arrive in western Crete , stepping directly off the aircraft into the warmth of the Aegean, with the Venetian harbour, wild Samaria Gorge, and the turquoise beaches of Elafonisi and Balos within easy reach. Good to know

Abu Dhabi to Chania. Everything you need to know.

How Much Does It Cost to Charter a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Chania?+
Chartering a private jet from Abu Dhabi to Chania (CHQ) typically costs between approximately $38,000 and $70,000 one-way. A super-midsize jet such as the Challenger 350 covers the 3,200 km sector direct, starting from around $38,000. A large-cabin jet such as the Gulfstream G450, offering premium comfort, ranges from $55,000 to $70,000. Key pricing variables include Greek airspace and a private airport operator handling fees at CHQ, seasonal summer demand (Crete is heavily trafficked by private aviation June, September), and aircraft positioning on the Gulf, eastern Mediterranean corridor.
Which Airports Are Used for Private Jet Flights Between Abu Dhabi and Chania?+
From Abu Dhabi, Al Bateen Executive Airport (AZI / OMAD) is the preferred departure hub; Zayed International Airport (AUH / OMAA) accommodates larger aircraft. In Chania, the gateway is Chania International Airport 'Daskalogiannis' (CHQ / LGSA), named after the 18th-century Cretan rebel hero, located 14 km from Chania city on the Akrotiri peninsula near Souda Bay. Operated by a private airport operator, it is a joint civil, military airport featuring a single runway capable of accommodating midsize, large-cabin, and some heavy jets, with private aviation handling available through a private airport operator's facilities. For eastern Crete and Heraklion, Heraklion International Airport (HER / LGIR) is the alternative gateway.
Which Aircraft Models Are Recommended for Private Jet Flights from Abu Dhabi to Chania?+
The Abu Dhabi, Chania sector (3,200 km) is well suited to large-cabin jets and super-midsize jets:

1. Gulfstream G450 (Large-Cabin Jet) , Range 7,800 km; comfortable non-stop; stand-up cabin for up to 14 passengers with full galley.
2. Challenger 350 (Super-Midsize Jet) , Range 5,900 km; direct non-stop; flat-floor cabin for up to 10 passengers; the most cost-effective option on this route.
3. Dassault Falcon 2000LXS , Range 7,400 km; fuel-efficient; proven into Aegean island airfields.

A dedicated Private Aviation Advisor will match the right aircraft for your group.
What Is the Typical Flight Duration for a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Chania?+
The direct flight time between Abu Dhabi (AZI / AUH) and Chania (CHQ) is approximately 4 hours to 4 hours 30 minutes on a large-cabin or super-midsize jet, depending on aircraft type and winds. Departing from Al Bateen, passengers arrive just 20 minutes before departure. On arrival at CHQ, a private airport operator's private handling ensures efficient clearance; Chania's iconic Venetian harbour and old town are approximately 20 minutes by chauffeured vehicle; the beaches of Elafonisi are approximately 75 minutes.
Are There Night Flight Restrictions at Chania Airport?+
Chania International Airport (CHQ / LGSA) is a joint civil, military facility and does not impose a hard public night-flight ban, but as a a private airport operator-operated airport, late-night private jet movements require advance coordination with handlers to confirm staffing and customs availability. Greek HCAA slot coordination applies during peak summer season. Al Bateen Executive Airport operates 24/7. Our operations team manages all advance arrangements as standard.
How Can I Arrange Ground Transfers After Landing at Chania Airport?+
We coordinate premium chauffeured transfers from the aircraft steps at CHQ. Chania's old town and waterfront tavernas are 20 minutes; the sandy beaches of Falasarna and Elafonisi 75 to 90 minutes; and Balos Lagoon approximately 90 minutes. For guests staying at Villa Vedema, Domes Noruz Chania, or a private villa estate in the Akrotiri peninsula, we pre-arrange full ramp-to-villa logistics. Helicopter transfers to any Cretan destination can also be coordinated.
What Is the Luggage Capacity on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Chania?+
On a large-cabin jet, the aft hold accommodates 10 to 14 suitcases plus cabin bags. On a super-midsize jet, hold capacity is 8 to 12 bags. Our advisors confirm all specifications at booking.
Can My Pets Travel with Me on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Chania?+
Absolutely , your cats and dogs travel in the cabin with you. Greece is an EU member state: pets require microchipping, rabies vaccination, and a valid EU-format animal health certificate; UAE-origin pets may additionally need a rabies antibody titre test with several weeks' lead time. Our ground team provides a full briefing and coordinates with CHQ handlers for any arrival veterinary checks.
