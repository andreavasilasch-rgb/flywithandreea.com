The route

Abu Dhabi → Zhuliany.

Chartering a private jet from Abu Dhabi to Zhuliany, the close-in general aviation and city airport serving Kyiv, provides a direct and secure connection between the UAE capital and Ukraine's capital city. Our aviation advisors are available 24/7 to assess current operational conditions on this routing and source the appropriate aircraft for your mission. As operational circumstances in Ukrainian airspace require continuous monitoring and specialist expertise, our flight operations team conducts detailed pre-flight assessments for every Kyiv-bound mission, ensuring your safety and regulatory compliance throughout.

Good to know

Abu Dhabi to Zhuliany. Everything you need to know.

How Much Does It Cost To Charter A Private Jet Between Abu Dhabi And Zhuliany?+
A private jet charter from Abu Dhabi to Kyiv Zhuliany costs from approximately €65,000 to €130,000 when operationally viable, reflecting the approximately 3,700 km routing, the current premium applicable to Ukrainian airspace operations, the specialised insurance and permit requirements involved, and the large cabin aircraft appropriate to this sector. Pricing variables include aircraft positioning, the operator's current risk assessment framework for operations into Ukraine, fuel at Abu Dhabi and any intermediate technical stop, applicable overflight fees for the multiple airspaces crossed, and the availability of crew with specific operational clearances for this routing.
What Are The Best Airports For Private Flights Between Abu Dhabi And Zhuliany?+
Abu Dhabi International Airport (AUH, OMAA) provides full private aviation terminal services for departure. Kyiv Zhuliany Airport (IEV, UKKK) is the city centre airport located approximately 8 km southwest of the Kyiv city centre, historically the preferred private aviation gateway for Kyiv due to its proximity to government, diplomatic, and commercial districts. Kyiv Boryspil International Airport (KBP, UKBB), approximately 29 km east of the city centre, is the larger international gateway and also serves private aviation. Current operational viability at both airports is subject to the ongoing security situation in Ukraine and must be assessed individually for each mission by our specialist operations team in coordination with the relevant aviation authorities.
Which Aircraft Models Are Most Frequently Chartered Between Abu Dhabi And Zhuliany?+
When the routing is operationally approved, large cabin aircraft such as the Bombardier Global 6000, Gulfstream G550, and Dassault Falcon 7X are appropriate for the approximately 3,700 km sector between Abu Dhabi and Kyiv. These aircraft provide the range, reliability, and cabin environment suited to this demanding mission profile. A Private Aviation Advisor will conduct the operational feasibility assessment and confirm aircraft availability for the specific dates and routing requested.
What Is The Typical Flight Duration Between Abu Dhabi And Zhuliany?+
When operationally cleared, a private jet flight from Abu Dhabi (AUH) to Kyiv Zhuliany (IEV) takes approximately 5 to 6 hours 30 minutes depending on the aircraft selected, routing, and current airspace availability. The private terminal at Abu Dhabi enables departure just 15 to 20 minutes after FBO arrival, with full onboard services throughout the mission. Our flight operations team continuously monitors NOTAM activity, airspace restrictions, and aeronautical information applicable to the routing for every Kyiv-bound mission.
Are There Any Night Flight Or Operational Restrictions For The Abu Dhabi To Zhuliany Route?+
Abu Dhabi International Airport (AUH) operates continuously without curfew. Kyiv Zhuliany Airport (IEV, UKKK) and Boryspil International Airport (KBP, UKBB) are subject to operational conditions that must be assessed on a mission-by-mission basis by our specialist flight operations team in coordination with Ukrainian authorities and relevant airspace managers. All operations into Ukraine require thorough pre-mission planning incorporating the most current aeronautical, security, and diplomatic information available. Our team will advise on current operational viability and any restrictions applicable to your specific travel dates before confirming the booking.
How Can I Arrange Ground Transfers When Flying Private Between Abu Dhabi And Zhuliany?+
We arrange professional ground transportation at both ends of the mission as part of the complete charter arrangement. Departing Abu Dhabi, a chauffeur service collects you from your residence or hotel. On arrival at Kyiv Zhuliany, the close proximity of the airport to the city centre, approximately 8 km from the Maidan Nezalezhnosti and government quarter, makes it the preferred private aviation gateway for time-sensitive diplomatic or executive arrivals in Kyiv. Our ground logistics team coordinates vehicles, security arrangements, and any necessary escort services in Kyiv appropriate to the nature of your mission.
What Is The Luggage Capacity When Chartering A Private Jet From Abu Dhabi To Zhuliany?+
Large cabin jets suitable for this routing, such as the Gulfstream G550 or Bombardier Global 6000, typically accommodate between 12 and 18 standard suitcases in dedicated cargo holds in addition to cabin baggage. For missions involving the transport of diplomatic materials, humanitarian supplies, or specialist equipment, the Global 6000's pressurised cargo hold provides a secure and climate-controlled environment. Clients with specific cargo requirements should detail these to our operations team at the time of enquiry so that aircraft selection and any additional handling procedures can be confirmed.
Can I Travel With My Pet On A Private Jet Between Abu Dhabi And Zhuliany?+
Companion animals including cats, dogs, and other pets are welcome in the cabin on private charter flights and travel far more comfortably on long sectors than in commercial cargo arrangements. For travel from the UAE to Ukraine, Ukrainian veterinary import regulations require a valid international health certificate endorsed by UAE government veterinary authorities, current rabies vaccination documentation, ISO-standard microchip identification, and compliance with Ukrainian State Food Safety and Consumer Protection Service procedures on arrival. Our team prepares the documentation requirements applicable to the current UAE to Ukraine regulatory framework and confirms all requirements are met prior to departure.
