How much does it cost to charter a private jet from Aberdeen to Bari?+
Charter pricing on the Aberdeen to Bari route typically ranges from €12,500 to €27,000 for a midsize jet, reflecting the 2,400 km sector and Bari Airport's operational characteristics. Key pricing variables include aircraft category and group size, seasonal demand peaks (July through September when Puglia's tourism demand peaks across private aviation), aircraft positioning costs from Scotland, landing and handling fees at Bari Karol Wojtyła Airport's business aviation apron, in-flight catering for the three-hour sector, and crew overnight expenses for extended Puglian stays. For super-midsize or large-cabin groups, rates rise to €20,000 to €36,000. Fly with Andreea provides all-inclusive, transparent quotations.
What airports serve private jet travel between Aberdeen and Bari?+
Aberdeen Airport (ABZ / EGPD) handles UK departures with a general aviation facility and private customs. Bari Karol Wojtyła Airport (BRI / LIBD) serves Puglia with a general aviation apron handling private jet movements through local ground handlers, with private customs facilitation and ramp-side vehicle coordination. BRI is located 9 km north-west of Bari city centre and provides convenient access to the historic Bari Vecchia, the Eataly market, and the Puglia road network. Brindisi Airport (BDS / LIBR), 113 km south-east of Bari, is an alternative for clients targeting the Salento peninsula, the Torre Guaceto reserve, or the Adriatic beaches around Ostuni. For clients heading to the Amalfi or Cilento coasts, Salerno to Pontecagnano Airport (QSR / LIRI) is also an option. Your Fly with Andreea advisor will recommend the optimal airport for your specific Puglia destination.

What is the typical private jet flight time from Aberdeen to Bari?+
Aberdeen to Bari covers approximately 2,400 km, with typical block times of two hours forty minutes to three hours fifteen minutes for midsize jets routing south over the UK and France, crossing the Alps or routing via the Adriatic. Passengers using Aberdeen's FBO arrive 15 to 20 minutes before departure no commercial check-in, no shared security, no boarding queues. At Bari Airport's general aviation apron, private Italian customs clearance takes under 10 minutes, with vehicles waiting for the 15-minute transfer to Bari's old city or the 30-to-90 minute drives to Alberobello, Ostuni, Lecce, or your Puglian masseria.
Are there night curfews or seasonal restrictions at Bari Airport for private jets?+
Bari Karol Wojtyła Airport (BRI) operates on a schedule broadly accommodating to private jet movements throughout the day and into the evening, with no formal published night curfew equivalent to northern European hub airports. Movements after 23:00 local time should be confirmed with the handling agent in advance for staffing availability. Aberdeen Airport (ABZ) has no night curfew for business aviation. During peak summer months (July and August), Bari Airport's general aviation apron fills rapidly Fly with Andreea books parking slots and handling services well in advance for summer Puglia itineraries to ensure preferred arrival windows are secured. Alternative aprons at Brindisi (BDS) or Taranto-Grottaglie (TAR) can be used if Bari is full during peak periods.

Can I bring my dog or cat on a private jet from Aberdeen to Bari?+
Your beloved companions are warmly welcome in the cabin with you on this two-and-a-half to three-hour route to Puglia. Italy, as an EU member state, applies the EU Pet Travel Scheme for animals arriving from the UK. Required documentation includes: an animal health certificate (AHC) completed by a UK Official Veterinarian (OV) within 10 days of travel; a valid microchip conforming to ISO 11784/11785; a current rabies vaccination administered at least 21 days before first EU entry for previously unvaccinated animals; and for dogs, an Echinococcus (tapeworm) treatment certificate issued between 24 and 120 hours before arrival. Bari Airport's general aviation area handles private-flight pet arrivals routinely. Fly with Andreea will provide the current AHC template and connect you with an accredited OV in your area. We recommend beginning documentation at least 72 hours before your departure date.
