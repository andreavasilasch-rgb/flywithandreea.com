Abu Dhabi → Zakinthos.

Chartering a private jet from Abu Dhabi to Zakinthos, the jewel of the Ionian Islands, offers an unparalleled way to arrive at one of Greece's most iconic island destinations. Our aviation experts are available 24/7 to identify the most suitable aircraft for this approximately 3,200 km journey, whether your purpose is a family summer retreat, a superyacht rendezvous in Zante harbour, or a private villa stay on the island. Good to know

Abu Dhabi to Zakinthos. Everything you need to know.

How Much Does It Cost To Charter A Private Jet Between Abu Dhabi And Zakinthos?+
A private jet charter from Abu Dhabi to Zakinthos costs from approximately €55,000 to €105,000, reflecting the approximately 3,200 km routing and the midsize to large cabin aircraft specification required for comfortable range performance. Zakinthos is a premium destination during the Greek summer season, which can affect both charter pricing and aircraft availability on the Abu Dhabi to Ionian corridor. Pricing variables include aircraft positioning, seasonal demand, fuel costs, Greek handling and landing fees at Zakinthos, and overflight permits for the airspaces crossed.
What Are The Best Airports For Private Flights Between Abu Dhabi And Zakinthos?+
Abu Dhabi International Airport (AUH, OMAA) provides a full-service private aviation terminal with round-the-clock handling. Zakinthos International Airport Dionysios Solomos (ZTH, LGZA) is the island's only airport, located approximately 6 km south of Zakinthos town, and handles both commercial and private aviation with seasonal handling services. The airport operates a shorter runway and limited apron space compared to major hubs, and slot availability during peak summer months must be secured well in advance. For larger aircraft or when Zakinthos slots are constrained, Kefalonia Island International Airport (EFL, LGKF) approximately 60 km to the north or Athens Eleftherios Venizelos International Airport (ATH, LGAV) with its comprehensive private aviation infrastructure can serve as alternative entry points with onward ground or sea transfers.
Which Aircraft Models Are Most Frequently Chartered Between Abu Dhabi And Zakinthos?+
In 2025, the Gulfstream G450, Bombardier Challenger 350, and Dassault Falcon 2000LXS were among the most popular aircraft for the Abu Dhabi to Ionian Islands sector. These platforms offer the range and cabin comfort appropriate to the approximately 5 to 6 hour mission duration, while remaining compatible with Zakinthos Airport's operational parameters. For families or larger groups, the Bombardier Challenger 605 provides additional passenger capacity and cargo volume. A Private Aviation Advisor will confirm aircraft-airport compatibility and recommend the optimal solution for your specific group.
What Is The Typical Flight Duration Between Abu Dhabi And Zakinthos?+
A private jet flight from Abu Dhabi (AUH) to Zakinthos (ZTH) takes approximately 5 to 6 hours 30 minutes depending on the aircraft selected and prevailing Mediterranean wind patterns. The private terminal at Abu Dhabi allows boarding just 15 to 20 minutes after FBO arrival, and the complete absence of connecting flights or commercial hub transits means your door-to-door time to the Ionian coast is dramatically shorter than any published commercial itinerary. Custom catering, preferred cabin temperature, and personalised entertainment throughout the flight ensure arrival in Zakinthos refreshed and ready for the island experience.
Are There Any Night Flight Or Operational Restrictions For The Abu Dhabi To Zakinthos Route?+
Abu Dhabi International Airport (AUH) operates 24 hours without curfew for private aviation. Zakinthos International Airport (ZTH, LGZA) operates on a seasonal schedule and has published hours that may be restricted during off-peak periods and extended during the summer tourism season. Night operations at Zakinthos require advance coordination with the Hellenic Civil Aviation Authority (HCAA) and the local airport management. For late-night arrivals on the island, our advisors may recommend routing through Athens Eleftherios Venizelos (ATH, LGAV), which operates continuously, followed by an early morning short sector to Zakinthos when the airport resumes full operations.
How Can I Arrange Ground Transfers When Flying Private Between Abu Dhabi And Zakinthos?+
We coordinate luxury ground transportation at both ends of the journey as a fully integrated part of your charter arrangement. Departing from Abu Dhabi, a chauffeur-driven vehicle collects you from your home or hotel and delivers you to the private terminal. On arrival at Zakinthos, a waiting vehicle transfers you to your villa estate, the Porto Zante Villas and Spa on Marathonisi beach, or directly to the harbour for your superyacht or private motorboat. For clients wishing to explore multiple Ionian islands during their stay, we can arrange private boat charters or helicopter transfers to neighbouring Kefalonia, Ithaca, or Lefkada.
What Is The Luggage Capacity When Chartering A Private Jet From Abu Dhabi To Zakinthos?+
Midsize and large cabin jets appropriate to this route, such as the Bombardier Challenger 350 or Gulfstream G450, typically accommodate between 8 and 16 standard suitcases in dedicated baggage compartments in addition to cabin items. For families travelling with beach and water sports equipment, snorkelling gear, or significant personal luggage for extended Ionian island stays, the Bombardier Challenger 605 provides superior cargo capacity. Clients carrying oversized items such as diving equipment or golf bags should advise our team at enquiry stage to allow accurate aircraft capacity confirmation.
Can I Travel With My Pet On A Private Jet Between Abu Dhabi And Zakinthos?+
Your dogs, cats, and other beloved companions are genuinely welcome in the cabin with you on a private charter, making island destinations like Zakinthos perfectly accessible for the whole family. For travel from the UAE to Greece, an EU member state, Greek veterinary regulations require an EU pet passport or an official third-country health certificate endorsed by UAE government veterinary authorities, proof of rabies vaccination within the applicable validity period, ISO-standard microchip identification, and compliance with Hellenic customs veterinary inspection procedures. Our team prepares the comprehensive documentation checklist for your specific travel dates and coordinates with veterinary authorities in both countries to ensure your four-legged family members arrive on the island of Zante without any border formality complications.
