How Much Does It Cost to Charter a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Alghero?+
Chartering a private jet from Abu Dhabi to Alghero (AHO) typically costs between approximately $45,000 and $90,000 one-way. At 4,640 km, this distance exceeds the range of light jets and stretches midsize jets to their limit; a super-midsize jet such as the Challenger 350 or Citation Longitude may require a short fuel stop, starting from around $45,000. A large-cabin jet such as the Gulfstream G450 or Bombardier Global 5000 can complete the route non-stop and offers superior cabin comfort, ranging from $65,000 to $90,000. Key pricing variables include aircraft positioning across the UAE, Mediterranean corridor, Italian and Sardinian airspace handling fees, seasonal peak demand (Sardinian summer season from June to August drives significant price increases), and AHO's runway constraints that restrict the largest aircraft categories.
Which Airports Are Used for Private Jet Flights Between Abu Dhabi and Alghero?+
From Abu Dhabi, the preferred private aviation gateway is Al Bateen Executive Airport (AZI / OMAD), with four FBOs and exclusive business-jet operations. For larger long-range aircraft, Zayed International Airport (AUH / OMAA) provides the necessary runway length and infrastructure. In northern Sardinia, Alghero, Fertilia Airport (AHO / LIEA) , also known as Alghero Riviera del Corallo Airport , is located 8 km north-northwest of Alghero city centre. It is operated by SOGEAAL and features a single runway of approximately 2,450 m, suited to light jets, midsize jets, and super-midsize jets; note that the runway length imposes practical limitations on the largest heavy jets, and aircraft selection should be confirmed against AHO's performance requirements. Sardinia's other airports , Olbia Costa Smeralda (OLB / LIEO) in the north-east and Cagliari Elmas (CAG / LIEE) in the south , are also served by our team and can be considered if charter flexibility or itinerary logistics require.
Which Aircraft Models Are Recommended for Private Jet Flights from Abu Dhabi to Alghero?+
The Abu Dhabi, Alghero sector (4,640 km) requires aircraft with meaningful range and a comfortable cabin for a flight of 5.5 to 7 hours. Three well-matched options are:

1. Challenger 350 (Super-Midsize Jet) , Range 5,900 km; may complete the sector direct with favourable winds; flat-floor cabin for up to 10 passengers; one of the most popular charter jets on Middle East, Mediterranean routes.
2. Gulfstream G450 (Large-Cabin Jet) , Range 7,800 km; guarantees a non-stop service with a premium stand-up cabin for up to 14 passengers and full galley.
3. Dassault Falcon 7X (Large-Cabin Trijet) , Range 11,000 km; exceptional comfort and proven performance into shorter airfields including AHO; ideal for passengers prioritising cabin environment on this leisure route.

A dedicated Private Aviation Advisor will match the best aircraft to your group size, schedule, and runway requirements at AHO.
What Is the Typical Flight Duration for a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Alghero?+
On a non-stop large-cabin jet such as the Gulfstream G450 or Falcon 7X, the Abu Dhabi (AZI / AUH) to Alghero (AHO) flight time is approximately 6 hours to 6 hours 30 minutes depending on aircraft type, routing, and wind conditions. On a super-midsize jet requiring a brief technical stop , for example, at Cairo, Athens, or Rome , total block time is approximately 7 hours 30 minutes to 8 hours 30 minutes including the ground stop. Departing from Al Bateen Executive Airport, passengers can arrive just 20 minutes before wheels-up, bypassing all commercial terminal processes. On arrival in Alghero, the relaxed pace of the Sardinian airport means passengers are through customs and in a vehicle within 20 minutes , with the old town's medieval walls, seafront restaurants, and luxury villas accessible in under 15 minutes from the terminal.
Are There Night Flight Restrictions at Alghero Airport?+
Alghero, Fertilia Airport (AHO / LIEA) does not currently impose a comprehensive night-flight ban, but as a medium-sized regional Italian airport, late-night private jet operations require prior coordination with the airport authority and ground handlers to confirm tower hours and staffing availability. Slot coordination for arrivals and departures outside standard operating hours should be arranged well in advance through our operations team. Al Bateen Executive Airport in Abu Dhabi operates 24/7, providing complete departure flexibility. Italy's ENAC (Ente Nazionale per l'Aviazione Civile) may impose specific procedures for night-time general-aviation operations; our operations desk manages all relevant notifications and permits as standard.
How Can I Arrange Ground Transfers After Landing at Alghero Airport?+
Alghero Airport's position 8 km from the city means a chauffeured Mercedes V-Class or luxury convertible can reach the historic Catalan-influenced old town, the Riviera del Corallo seafront, or the stunning beaches of Lazzaretto and Maria the national carrier in under 15 minutes. For guests staying at the Villa Las Tronas , Alghero's iconic seafront palazzo hotel , or at one of the region's exclusive private villas, we pre-arrange a ramp-side handover ensuring a seamless transition from aircraft to final destination. For the most spectacular arrival experience, we can arrange a private boat transfer from the marina, allowing guests to approach their villa or yacht directly from the sea , a uniquely Sardinian welcome that begins the island experience the moment you step off the jet.
What Is the Luggage Capacity on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Alghero?+
On a super-midsize jet such as the Challenger 350, the aft baggage compartment typically holds 8 to 12 standard suitcases plus cabin bags , ideal for a family holiday with generous luggage or leisure guests with water sports equipment. On a large-cabin jet such as the Gulfstream G450, hold capacity increases to 10 to 14 bags. If you are bringing oversized items , diving gear, surfboards, or sailing equipment , the Falcon 7X's dedicated baggage hold and large loading door make it particularly practical. Our advisors confirm exact hold dimensions at the time of booking to ensure no compromise on luggage.
Can I Travel with Pets on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Alghero?+
Of course , your dogs and cats travel in the cabin with you, resting comfortably at your feet or in their beds throughout the flight, arriving in Sardinia relaxed and ready to enjoy the island alongside you. Italy is an EU member state and applies EU pet travel regulations: pets must be microchipped, vaccinated against rabies, and accompanied by an EU-format animal health certificate or equivalent third-country health certificate. Pets entering from the UAE may also require a rabies antibody titre test, and lead times for certification can be several weeks. Our ground coordination team will brief you on the full current requirements well in advance and work with handlers at AHO to facilitate any veterinary checks on arrival , ensuring the process is entirely seamless for you and your beloved companions.
