The route

Abu Dhabi → La Palma.

A private jet from Abu Dhabi to La Palma connects the UAE capital with the most dramatically beautiful of the Canary Islands , a volcanic island of breathtaking contrasts, from the ancient Caldera de Taburiente crater , one of the largest in the world , to the extraordinary stargazing at the Roque de los Muchachos Observatory (one of the world's premier astronomical sites), the Silk Route trade-wind forests of the Biosphere Reserve, and the colourful colonial capital of Santa Cruz de la Palma. Good to know

Abu Dhabi to La Palma. Everything you need to know.

How Much Does It Cost to Charter a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to La Palma?+
Chartering a private jet from Abu Dhabi to La Palma (SPC) typically costs between approximately $88,000 and $148,000 one-way , one of the longer private jet routes from Abu Dhabi. At over 7,100 km, the sector requires at least one technical stop (typically in Madrid, Lisbon, or Tenerife). Key pricing variables include limited handling infrastructure at the small La Palma airport, aircraft positioning, and the substantial total sector distance. For most groups, the recommended approach is Abu Dhabi → Gran Canaria (LPA) or Tenerife Sur (TFS) , both well-equipped for large-cabin jets , followed by a 30-minute domestic the inter-island carrier connector to La Palma. Our advisors will identify the most practical routing for your requirements.
Which Airports Are Used for Private Jet Flights Between Abu Dhabi and La Palma?+
From Abu Dhabi, Zayed International Airport (AUH / OMAA) is preferred for ultra-long-range jets. In La Palma, the gateway is La Palma Airport (SPC / GCLA), known officially as 'Santa Cruz de la Palma Airport', located 8 km south of the island capital Santa Cruz de la Palma. Its shorter runway limits large-jet operations; Gran Canaria Airport (LPA / GCLP) and Tenerife South (TFS / GCTS) are the best large-jet alternatives with 30-minute the inter-island carrier domestic connections to La Palma. a direct scheduled service flies Madrid, Abu Dhabi, and a European carrier connects La Palma to Gran Canaria, making a Madrid or Gran Canaria transit the standard routing for Gulf passengers.
Which Aircraft Models Are Recommended for Private Jet Flights from Abu Dhabi to La Palma?+
The Abu Dhabi, La Palma sector (7,100+ km) is among the longest from the Gulf, requiring ultra-long-range aircraft:

1. Gulfstream G650ER , Range 12,960 km; one European stop; sleeping suites; best choice for direct large-cabin access.
2. Bombardier Global 7500 , Range 14,260 km; cabin equivalent to a full apartment; fewest stops.
3. Practical alternative: Abu Dhabi → Gran Canaria (LPA) by G450 with stop, then the inter-island carrier 30-min connector to SPC.

A dedicated Private Aviation Advisor will design the optimal routing.
What Is the Typical Flight Duration for a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to La Palma?+
With one technical stop in Europe, the Abu Dhabi (AUH) to La Palma (SPC) total block time is approximately 7.8 to 9.1 hours. Via the Gran Canaria connector routing, the journey is approximately 7.8 to 9.1 hours total (including the 30-minute domestic leg). On arrival, Santa Cruz de la Palma and the colonial waterfront are approximately 10 minutes from the airport; the Caldera de Taburiente viewpoint approximately 30 km; and the Roque de los Muchachos Observatory (2,426 m altitude) approximately 45 km north.
Are There Night Flight Restrictions at La Palma Airport?+
La Palma Airport (SPC / GCLA) has restricted operating hours as a regional AENA facility. Gran Canaria (LPA) and Tenerife South (TFS) are the preferred alternatives for unrestricted operations. Our operations team manages all ENAIRE/AENA coordination as standard.
How Can I Arrange Ground Transfers After Landing at La Palma Airport?+
We coordinate premium chauffeured transfers from the airport. Santa Cruz de la Palma is 10 minutes; the Caldera de Taburiente viewpoints approximately 35 minutes; the astronomical observatory at Roque de los Muchachos approximately 45 minutes on winding mountain roads. Helicopter transfers to the volcano caldera rim or the north of the island are available on request.
What Is the Luggage Capacity on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to La Palma?+
On an ultra-long-range jet, the aft hold accommodates 12 to 20 bags. On the domestic connector to SPC, baggage is limited; we advise in advance. Our advisors confirm all specifications.
Can My Pets Travel with Me on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to La Palma?+
Yes , on the private jet leg. Spain/Canary Islands (EU): microchipping, rabies vaccination, and a valid EU-format animal health certificate required; UAE-origin pets may need a titre test. On the the inter-island carrier domestic connector, pet policies apply. Our ground team provides a full briefing.
