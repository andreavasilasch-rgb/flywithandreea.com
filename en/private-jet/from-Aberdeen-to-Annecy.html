The route

Aberdeen → Annecy.

Annecy the Jewel of the French Alps, with its turquoise lake, medieval old town, and effortless access to Chamonix, Megève, and Courchevel's ski resorts lies approximately 1,500 km from Aberdeen and is reachable in under two hours aboard a light or midsize private jet. This is one of Scotland's most popular Alpine private aviation routes, serving ski season clients from January through April, summer lake-sailing enthusiasts from June to August, and year-round business travellers with commercial interests in the Haute-Savoie technology and precision-manufacturing corridor. Good to know

Aberdeen to Annecy. Everything you need to know.

How much does it cost to charter a private jet from Aberdeen to Annecy?+
Charter pricing on the Aberdeen to Annecy route typically ranges from €8,500 to €18,000 for a light or midsize jet, with the shorter sector length keeping costs well within the reach of frequent business travellers and leisure groups alike. Pricing variables include peak ski-season surcharges (January through March when Annecy/Chambéry demand is at its highest), aircraft positioning if the jet originates outside Scotland, landing and handling fees at Annecy Haute-Savoie Mont-Blanc Airport, in-flight catering specifications for the two-hour journey, and crew overnight requirements for multi-day ski trips. For larger groups of six or more requiring a midsize or super-midsize cabin, rates range from €13,000 to €24,000. Fly with Andreea provides all-inclusive, transparent quotations.
What airports are used for private jet travel between Aberdeen and Annecy?+
Aberdeen Airport (ABZ / EGPD) handles departures with a general aviation facility, private customs, and direct airside access. Annecy Haute-Savoie Mont-Blanc Airport (NCY / LFLP) is the preferred arrival point a charming small airport 4 km south of Annecy town, handling light and midsize jets with a general aviation apron and local ground handling. The runway (2,200 m) accepts aircraft up to the Citation Longitude and Phenom 300E class comfortably. For larger-cabin jets or groups requiring greater aircraft availability, Geneva Airport (GVA / LSGG) and Chambéry-Savoie Airport (CMF / LFLB) are the established Alpine-region alternatives, both within 45 to 60 minutes of central Annecy by private car and commonly used during peak ski season. Your Fly with Andreea advisor will confirm the optimal airport for your aircraft type and seasonal slot availability.
Which private jets are most popular for the Aberdeen to Annecy route?+
At 1,500 km, the Aberdeen to Annecy sector is ideally matched to light and midsize jets. The Embraer Phenom 300E is the most-requested aircraft on Alpine routes from Scotland in 2025, combining a nine-passenger cabin, 3,650 km range, and strong performance into Annecy's 2,200 m runway. The Cessna Citation CJ4 is a cost-efficient alternative for groups of four to six, with 2,850 km range and proven Alpine airfield performance. For groups preferring a stand-up cabin on the two-hour flight, the Embraer Legacy 450 or Cessna Citation Longitude are the midsize choices, offering wider cabins and substantially larger luggage holds suited to ski boot bags, helmet cases, and winter wardrobes. A Fly with Andreea advisor will recommend the right aircraft for your group, departure season, and luggage profile.
What is the typical private jet flight time between Aberdeen and Annecy?+
Aberdeen to Annecy covers approximately 1,500 km, placing block times between one hour forty-five minutes and two hours fifteen minutes for light and midsize jets. Passengers using Aberdeen's FBO arrive 15 to 20 minutes before departure, with complete flexibility on luggage, including ski equipment cases loaded directly into the hold. At Annecy Airport, the compact general aviation apron means passengers are through French customs and in their private vehicle within minutes of landing with Annecy's old town 10 minutes by car, the Megève resort 50 minutes, Chamonix 75 minutes, and Courchevel 90 minutes for clients continuing into the Alps.
Are there seasonal or nighttime operational limits at Annecy Airport for private jets?+
Annecy Haute-Savoie Mont-Blanc Airport (NCY) operates with limited nighttime hours movements are generally restricted in the late evening and early morning, with the airport formally open from approximately 07:00 to 21:00 local time, subject to seasonal variation. Clients planning late-night arrivals during ski season should consider Geneva (GVA), which operates 24 hours, or Chambéry (CMF), as more flexible nighttime alternatives within comparable transfer distance to the main Savoie and Haute-Savoie resorts. Aberdeen Airport (ABZ) has no equivalent night curfew for business aviation. Fly with Andreea's operations team monitors NCY slot availability, coordinates with handling agents during peak season well in advance, and will advise alternative airports proactively if your preferred arrival window falls outside operational hours.
How do I arrange a luxury transfer from Annecy Airport to the lake, Megève, or a ski chalet in Chamonix?+
Upon landing at Annecy Airport (NCY), Fly with Andreea coordinates premium transfers through its French Alpine concierge partners. Transfers to Megève's Grand Hôtel du Mont-Blanc take approximately 50 minutes by private 4×4; to Chamonix's Place du Mont-Blanc, 75 minutes; and to Courchevel 1850's helipad (for winter helicopter connections), 90 minutes. For clients arriving with ski equipment, Fly with Andreea's drivers use vehicles with roof-rack ski carriers or extended cargo SUVs as standard during winter season. Helicopter connections from the Annecy area to Megève, Val d'Isère, or Verbier are bookable through our Alpine aviation network. All transfers confirmed and waiting at the apron before landing.
What luggage capacity is available on a private jet from Aberdeen to Annecy?+
Light jets such as the Phenom 300E and Citation CJ4 offer hold volumes of 400 to 700 litres, accommodating four to seven full-size suitcases and cabin bags adequate for most group sizes on a ski weekend or summer lake break. However, ski travel demands more hold space per person than typical leisure travel: ski bags, boot bags, helmet cases, and après-ski luggage add significant bulk. For this reason, many Aberdeen to Annecy ski parties select the midsize Embraer Legacy 450 or Citation Longitude, whose holds of 700 to 1,200 litres handle the full kit of six to nine winter travellers without compromise. The Pilatus PC-12 NG, with its rear cargo door and flexible interior, is an outstanding specialist option for mixed passenger-and-ski-equipment loads. Advise your Fly with Andreea coordinator of ski and sports equipment volumes at the time of booking.
Can I bring my dog or cat on a private jet from Aberdeen to Annecy?+
Absolutely your beloved companions are welcome in the cabin for the short Aberdeen to Annecy flight. France, as an EU member state, applies the EU Pet Travel Scheme for animals arriving from the UK. Required documentation includes: an animal health certificate (AHC) completed by a UK Official Veterinarian (OV) within 10 days of travel; a valid microchip conforming to ISO 11784/11785; a current rabies vaccination administered at least 21 days before first EU entry for previously unvaccinated animals; and for dogs, an Echinococcus (tapeworm) treatment certificate issued between 24 and 120 hours before arrival. French border officials at private airports routinely process pet arrivals on private aircraft. Fly with Andreea will provide the current AHC template and connect you with an accredited OV. We recommend beginning documentation at least 72 hours before your departure date.
