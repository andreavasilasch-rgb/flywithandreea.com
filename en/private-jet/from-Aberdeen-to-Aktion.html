The route

Aberdeen → Aktion.

Aktion the gateway airport for Preveza and the Ionian island of Lefkada is one of Greece's most desirable summer charter destinations, offering direct access to turquoise waters, superyacht anchorages, and whitewashed villages without the crowds of more commercially served islands. The approximately 2,700 km distance from Aberdeen is ideally suited to a midsize or large-cabin jet, completing the journey in three to three and a half hours. Fly with Andreea arranges private jet charters on this route for Scottish families, energy sector professionals, and yachting clients seeking a seamless, unhurried start to their Greek escape. Aberdeen to Aktion. Everything you need to know.

How much does it cost to charter a private jet from Aberdeen to Aktion?+
Charter pricing on the Aberdeen to Aktion route typically ranges from €14,000 to €32,000 depending on aircraft category, season, and departure flexibility. The 2,700 km sector is well within the operational envelope of midsize jets, though many clients prefer a large-cabin jet for added comfort on the three-to-three-and-a-half hour flight. Pricing variables include peak summer season surcharges (July and August when Lefkada yacht season peaks), aircraft positioning costs if the jet is based outside Scotland, landing and handling fees at Aktion National Airport, specific catering requests for the onboard journey, and crew overnight requirements if a return sector is planned within 24 hours. Contact a Fly with Andreea advisor for a fully transparent, all-inclusive quote.
What airports serve a private jet flight between Aberdeen and Aktion?+
Aberdeen Airport (ABZ / EGPD) handles departures with a dedicated general aviation facility, private customs clearance, and efficient airside access. Aktion National Airport (PVK / LGPZ) officially Preveza/Lefkada National Airport is the arrival airport, located on the Aktion peninsula approximately 5 km from Preveza town and 22 km from Lefkada island via the causeway. PVK handles private jet movements through its general aviation apron with local ground handling agents providing ramp coordination, customs clearance, and vehicle transfers. In the UK, clients with greater scheduling flexibility may also position through Edinburgh Airport (EDI / EGPH) or Glasgow International (GLA / EGPF). During peak season, advance slot booking at PVK is essential your Fly with Andreea advisor manages this from the outset.
Which aircraft models are most frequently used for private flights from Aberdeen to Aktion?+
The 2,700 km Aberdeen to Aktion sector is most efficiently served by midsize and super-midsize jets in 2025, balancing cabin comfort with operating cost on a three-to-three-and-a-half hour flight. The Cessna Citation Longitude offers a stand-up cabin, 2,700 km range with full fuel, and seating for up to 12 passengers making it ideally matched to this sector. The Embraer Praetor 600 is a strong alternative, offering exceptional range (7,900 km), a wide-body cabin feel in a super-midsize footprint, and quieter interior noise levels than many competitors. For groups of three to five seeking cost efficiency, the Hawker 900XP provides a proven, capable cabin with 5,400 kg luggage capacity on a route comfortably within its range. A Fly with Andreea advisor will recommend the right aircraft for your group size and preferred cabin experience.
What is the typical private jet flight time from Aberdeen to Aktion?+
Aberdeen to Aktion (PVK) covers approximately 2,700 km, with a typical block time of three hours to three hours thirty minutes depending on aircraft type and wind conditions. Departing from Aberdeen's FBO, you arrive at the aircraft just 15 to 20 minutes before wheels-up with no public check-in, no baggage belt, and no shared departure lounge. The FBO provides catered private lounges, fresh newspapers and refreshments, and direct airside vehicle access. Upon landing at Aktion, the airport's compact layout means you are in your ground transport within minutes of engines shutting down, with Lefkada island accessible via the causeway in under 30 minutes and private yacht transfers from Lefkada Marina arranged to meet your schedule.
Are there any night curfews or operational restrictions at Aktion Airport?+
Aktion National Airport (PVK / LGPZ) operates seasonally, with full-service operations typically running from late April through October to serve Greek island demand, and reduced capacity in the winter months. During the operational season, PVK generally accommodates private jet movements during daylight and early evening hours, with late-night arrivals subject to ATC and handling availability. There is no formal published night curfew equivalent to major European hub airports, but private jet operators are advised to confirm handling availability for movements after 22:00 local time. Aberdeen Airport (ABZ) has no night curfew for business aviation. Fly with Andreea's operations team books slots, coordinates handling agents at both ends, and confirms operational windows well before departure particularly during peak July and August when PVK ramp capacity is at a premium.
How can I arrange a luxury transfer from Aktion Airport to Lefkada or a superyacht in Lefkada Marina?+
Upon landing at Aktion (PVK), Fly with Andreea coordinates transfers to meet every client's preferred Ionian itinerary. For Lefkada island, the drive across the causeway from the airport takes approximately 3.3 to 4.1 hours in a private vehicle, arriving directly at the island's most sought-after address Lefkada Town's marina, the hillside villas above Agios Nikitas, or the beachfront at Porto Katsiki. For superyacht clients, we liaise directly with your captain and marina team to synchronise your aircraft landing time with tender or dock transfer. Helicopter connections from PVK to Corfu, Kefalonia, or Mykonos for onward island-hopping are also available through our regional aviation partners. All transport is confirmed and waiting at the apron before your aircraft doors open.
How much luggage can I bring on a private jet from Aberdeen to Aktion?+
On midsize jets such as the Cessna Citation Longitude or Embraer Praetor 600 the most frequently chartered aircraft on this route external baggage compartments typically accommodate six to ten full-size suitcases plus cabin bags, sufficient for groups of four to ten passengers on a typical Greek island escape. If you are travelling with bulky items sailing bags, water sports equipment, multiple sets of luggage for extended Mediterranean cruising, or golf bags for the Lefkada resort courses the Embraer Praetor 600's 6.4 m³ hold offers exceptional capacity for its class. Fly with Andreea's team will confirm exact baggage volumes for your selected aircraft and advise on any item that requires advance declaration or specialist handling. There are no baggage fees and no restrictions comparable to commercial airline policies.
Can I bring my dog or cat on a private jet from Aberdeen to Aktion?+
Your beloved companions whether a loyal Spaniel, a beloved Siamese, or any other furry family member are welcome in the cabin with you on the Aberdeen to Aktion route. Greece is an EU member state and applies the EU Pet Travel Scheme for animals entering from the UK post-Brexit. Requirements include a valid animal health certificate (AHC) issued by a UK government-authorised veterinarian within 10 days of travel; a microchip implanted before or simultaneously with a rabies vaccination; a valid rabies vaccination administered at least 21 days before first entry into the EU (for previously unvaccinated animals); and a tapeworm treatment certificate for dogs issued between 24 and 120 hours before arrival. Fly with Andreea's advisors will confirm the current AHC template and connect you with an OV (Official Veterinarian) in your area. Begin the process at least 72 hours before departure to keep the journey stress-free for both you and your four-legged family members.
