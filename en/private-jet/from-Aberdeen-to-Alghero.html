How much does it cost to charter a private jet from Aberdeen to Alghero?+
Charter pricing on the Aberdeen–Alghero route typically ranges from €12,000 to €26,000 for a midsize jet, reflecting the 2,100 km sector and the convenience of direct access to Sardinia without commercial stopover. Key pricing variables include seasonal demand peak summer rates (July to August) rise as Sardinian demand intensifies across the private aviation market aircraft positioning costs if the jet originates outside Scotland, landing and handling fees at Alghero-Fertilia Airport, catering specifications for the onboard journey, and crew overnight requirements for same-day returns. For larger groups preferring a super-midsize or large-cabin aircraft, rates rise to the €20,000 to €35,000 range. Fly with Andreea provides fully transparent, all-inclusive quotations.
What airports are used for private jet travel between Aberdeen and Alghero?+
Aberdeen Airport (ABZ / EGPD) provides the UK departure point with a general aviation terminal, private customs, and rapid airside access. Alghero-Fertilia Airport (AHO / LIEA) is the destination, serving north-west Sardinia with a well-equipped general aviation apron and ground handling services capable of accommodating business jets year-round. AHO sits approximately 12 km north of Alghero's historic centre and 30 km from the entrance to the Costa Smeralda coastal road. For clients connecting to Porto Cervo, Olbia Costa Smeralda Airport (OLB / LIEO) offers more direct access to the north-east of the island and is worth considering for yacht-based itineraries. Your Fly with Andreea advisor will recommend the optimal airport based on your final destination within Sardinia.

What is the typical private jet flight duration from Aberdeen to Alghero?+
Aberdeen to Alghero covers approximately 2,100 km, placing block times between two hours thirty minutes and three hours depending on aircraft performance and wind conditions. Passengers using Aberdeen's private FBO arrive at the aircraft just 15 to 20 minutes before departure bypassing public security lanes, check-in desks, and commercial boarding queues. The FBO provides a private lounge, freshly prepared catering, and direct airside vehicle transfer to the aircraft steps. At Alghero-Fertilia Airport, the compact general aviation area means passengers clear customs and reach ground transport within minutes of landing with Alghero's medieval centre, beach clubs, and marina all accessible within 15 to 25 minutes by private vehicle.
Are there night curfews or seasonal restrictions at Alghero Airport for private jet flights?+
Alghero-Fertilia Airport (AHO) operates on a seasonal schedule that aligns broadly with Mediterranean tourism demand, with full services typically available from May through October. Extended evening operations are generally accommodated for private jet arrivals, though movements after 22:00 local time should be confirmed with the handling agent in advance. There is no formal blanket night curfew equivalent to major hub airports, but late-night private jet arrivals are subject to ATC availability. Aberdeen Airport (ABZ) imposes no night curfew on general aviation. During peak summer months (July and August), Alghero's apron capacity fills rapidly Fly with Andreea books slots well in advance to secure preferred arrival windows and dedicated parking for your aircraft during Sardinian stops.

Fly with Andreea's team confirms exact hold dimensions for any specialist items. There are no commercial-style baggage fees, no arbitrary weight limits, and no shared baggage systems.
Can my dog or cat travel with me on a private jet from Aberdeen to Alghero?+
Your cherished companions are welcome in the cabin alongside you on this route to Sardinia. As Italy is an EU member state, UK-based pet owners travel under the EU Pet Travel Scheme post-Brexit. Required documentation includes: an animal health certificate (AHC) completed by a UK Official Veterinarian (OV) within 10 days of travel; a microchip implanted before or simultaneously with the rabies vaccination; a valid rabies vaccination administered at least 21 days before first travel into the EU (for previously unvaccinated animals); and for dogs, a tapeworm (Echinococcus) treatment certificate issued between 24 and 120 hours before arrival in Italy. Alghero Airport has established protocols for processing animals arriving on private flights. Fly with Andreea's team will provide a current AHC template and connect you with a local OV. We recommend initiating all documentation at least 72 hours before your departure date.
