The route

Abu Dhabi → Nuuk Airport.

A private jet from Abu Dhabi to Nuuk connects the UAE capital with the capital of the world's largest island: Greenland, an Arctic and sub-Arctic territory of staggering natural grandeur where the Ilulissat Icefjord calves colossal icebergs into Disko Bay, the Northern Lights paint the winter sky, the tundra blazes with Arctic flora in summer, and the indigenous Greenlandic Inuit culture is one of the most distinctive on earth. Nuuk itself, perched on a rugged peninsula above fjords and islands, is a compact city of coloured houses, the National Museum of Greenland, and a growing culinary scene celebrating musk ox and Arctic char. This is one of the most ambitious and remote private jet destinations from Abu Dhabi, typically routed via Iceland or Copenhagen. Our advisors manage all details 24/7.

Good to know

Abu Dhabi to Nuuk Airport. Everything you need to know.

How Much Does It Cost to Charter a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Nuuk?+
Chartering a private jet from Abu Dhabi to Nuuk (GOH) is a specialist expedition sector, typically costing between approximately $120,000 and $200,000 one-way. The routing typically stages via Reykjavik (KEF) or Copenhagen (CPH), with Nuuk Airport's recently extended runway now accommodating larger jet aircraft following its comprehensive 2019-2024 reconstruction. Total flight time across all legs is approximately 12 to 16 hours depending on routing and technical stops. Key pricing variables include the remote handling infrastructure at Nuuk, Arctic fuel pre-positioning, and complex crew logistics.
Which Airports Are Used for Private Jet Flights Between Abu Dhabi and Nuuk?+
From Abu Dhabi, Zayed International Airport (AUH / OMAA) is preferred for larger jets on this extended routing. In Nuuk, the gateway is Nuuk Airport, also called Mittarfik Nuuk (GOH / BGGH), the hub of Greenland's aviation network, operated by Greenland Airports. Originally constructed in 1979 for short take-off and landing aircraft, the airport was comprehensively rebuilt and expanded between 2019 and 2024, including a significant runway extension that now allows larger jet aircraft to serve the capital. Standard routing transits via Reykjavik Keflavik (KEF) or Copenhagen (CPH), which serve as the main gateways to Greenland.
Which Aircraft Models Are Recommended for Private Jet Flights from Abu Dhabi to Nuuk?+
The Abu Dhabi to Nuuk routing requires careful aircraft selection given the Arctic operating environment:

1. Gulfstream G650ER (Ultra-Long-Range Jet) - Range approximately 12,960 km; reaches Keflavik (KEF) in one leg from Abu Dhabi, then Nuuk from KEF.
2. Bombardier Global 5000 or 6000 - Range up to approximately 11,100 km; suitable for the KEF or CPH staging approach.
3. Gulfstream G450 - One or two technical stops; proven polar operator.

All Arctic operations require cold-weather aircraft preparation, crew Arctic briefings, and advance coordination with Greenland Airports. A dedicated Private Aviation Advisor will design the complete routing.
What Is the Typical Flight Duration for a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Nuuk?+
Via Reykjavik (KEF): Abu Dhabi to KEF is approximately 7 hours; KEF to Nuuk (GOH) is approximately 3 hours 30 minutes; total airborne time approximately 10 hours 30 minutes. Via Copenhagen (CPH): Abu Dhabi to CPH is approximately 7 hours; CPH to GOH is approximately 4 hours 30 minutes; total airborne time approximately 11 hours 30 minutes. Nuuk city centre and the National Museum are approximately 10 to 15 minutes from the airport; the Nuuk Fjord kayaking and boat tour departures approximately 20 minutes; and the Greenland Institute of Natural Resources (for expedition briefings) approximately 15 minutes.
Are There Special Operational Considerations for the Abu Dhabi to Nuuk Routing?+
Yes. Arctic operations require: cold-weather aircraft preparation and anti-icing coordination; crew Arctic survival briefings; advance Greenland Airports ground-handling reservation; SELCAL and polar HF radio capability; careful weather planning (Nuuk can experience severe and rapidly changing conditions); and fuel pre-positioning at Keflavik or Copenhagen. Our operations team manages all of these as standard for every Nuuk booking.
How Can I Arrange Ground Transfers After Landing at Nuuk Airport?+
We coordinate transfers from the airport. Nuuk city centre and the National Museum of Greenland are 10 to 15 minutes by road. For guests embarking on fjord expeditions, whale-watching tours, dog-sled experiences (winter), or helicopter transfers to the Greenland ice sheet, we coordinate all Arctic expedition logistics in partnership with specialist Greenlandic operators.
What Is the Luggage Capacity on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Nuuk?+
On a large-cabin jet, the aft hold accommodates 10 to 14 suitcases plus expedition equipment bags. For Arctic expeditions, all specialist gear dimensions and weights must be declared at booking.
Can My Pets Travel with Me on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Nuuk?+
Yes, pets may travel in the cabin. Greenland (Danish Kingdom): EU-equivalent pet travel requirements apply, including microchipping, rabies vaccination, and valid health certificates. Additional biosecurity requirements may apply for Arctic entry. Our ground team provides a full briefing.
