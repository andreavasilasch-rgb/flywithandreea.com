The route

Abu Dhabi → Palma Mallorca.

A private jet from Abu Dhabi to Palma de Mallorca connects the UAE capital with the Mediterranean's most sophisticated island capital, the gateway to the Balearic archipelago's extraordinary combination of natural beauty, luxury superyacht culture, pine-clad mountain scenery, private villa estates, and cosmopolitan leisure. Good to know

Abu Dhabi to Palma Mallorca. Everything you need to know.

How Much Does It Cost to Charter a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Palma Mallorca?+
Chartering a private jet from Abu Dhabi to Palma de Mallorca (PMI) typically costs between approximately $82,000 and $135,000 one-way. A large-cabin jet such as the Gulfstream G450 covers the sector with one technical stop, starting from around $85,000. Ultra-long-range jets offering near-non-stop capability range from $118,000 to $135,000+. Key pricing variables include AENA handling at Palma's Son San Joan Airport, strong peak summer demand (July to August) when the island is at maximum capacity, aircraft positioning, and demand from the island's substantial international luxury villa, superyacht, and real estate community.
Which Airports Are Used for Private Jet Flights Between Abu Dhabi and Palma Mallorca?+
From Abu Dhabi, Al Bateen Executive Airport (AZI / OMAD) is the preferred departure hub; Zayed International Airport (AUH / OMAA) accommodates larger aircraft. In Mallorca, the gateway is Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI / LEPA), also known as Son San Joan Airport, an AENA facility located approximately 8 km east of Palma city centre. Spain's third-busiest airport during summer, it handles both commercial and private aviation with dedicated FBO services. During peak season (July to August), the airport is extraordinarily busy with private jet movements and advance slot coordination is essential.
Which Aircraft Models Are Recommended for Private Jet Flights from Abu Dhabi to Palma Mallorca?+
The Abu Dhabi to Palma sector (approximately 6,800 km) requires long-range aircraft:

1. Gulfstream G450 (Large-Cabin Jet) - Range approximately 7,800 km; one technical stop; stand-up cabin for up to 14 passengers.
2. Bombardier Global 5000 (Ultra-Long-Range Jet) - Range approximately 9,600 km; near-non-stop; three-zone cabin.
3. Gulfstream G650ER - Full non-stop capability; maximum comfort for this 9-hour sector.

A dedicated Private Aviation Advisor will match the ideal aircraft.
What Is the Typical Flight Duration for a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Palma Mallorca?+
With one technical stop, the Abu Dhabi (AZI / AUH) to Palma (PMI) total block time is approximately 9 to 10 hours. Palma city centre and the cathedral are approximately 15 minutes from the airport; the superyacht marina at the Real Club Nautico approximately 15 minutes; Es Trenc beach on the south coast approximately 50 minutes; and the Serra de Tramuntana mountains at Valldemossa approximately 35 minutes north-west.
Are There Night Flight Restrictions at Palma Airport?+
Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI / LEPA) operates under AENA noise regulations; late-night movements are restricted. During peak season, the airport operates very high traffic volumes and advance slot coordination is essential. Our operations team manages all ENAIRE coordination and peak-season slot booking as standard.
How Can I Arrange Ground Transfers After Landing at Palma Airport?+
We coordinate premium chauffeured transfers from the airport. Palma city centre and La Seu cathedral are 15 minutes; the superyacht marina 15 minutes; Valldemossa and the Tramuntana mountains 35 minutes; Soller and the orange groves 45 minutes; and Es Trenc beach 50 minutes. For guests arriving by superyacht, we coordinate airport-to-tender connections at the Real Club Nautico or the Palma Marina.
What Is the Luggage Capacity on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Palma Mallorca?+
On a large-cabin jet, the aft hold accommodates 10 to 14 suitcases plus cabin bags. Our advisors confirm all specifications at booking.
Can My Pets Travel with Me on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Palma Mallorca?+
Yes, your cats and dogs travel in the cabin. Spain/Balearic Islands (EU): microchipping, rabies vaccination, and a valid EU-format animal health certificate are required; UAE-origin pets may need a titre test. Our ground team coordinates with PMI handlers.
