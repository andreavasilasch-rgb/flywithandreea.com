The route

Abu Dhabi → Dammam.

A private jet from Abu Dhabi to Dammam is the fastest and most efficient connection between the UAE capital and Saudi Arabia's Eastern Province , the kingdom's oil and gas heartland, home to Saudi Aramco's global headquarters, the King Fahd International Airport, and the industrial powerhouses of Jubail and Ras Tanura. At approximately 430 km, this Gulf hop is among the shortest international private jet routes available, delivering passengers in under 45 minutes. Our aviation advisors are available 24/7 to arrange your charter and manage all Saudi GACA permits.

Good to know

Abu Dhabi to Dammam. Everything you need to know.

How Much Does It Cost to Charter a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Dammam?+
Chartering a private jet from Abu Dhabi to Dammam (DMM) typically costs between approximately $11,000 and $20,000 one-way. A light jet such as the Phenom 300E covers the 430 km sector in under 40 minutes, starting from around $11,000. A midsize jet such as the Citation XLS+ offers greater cabin space for executive delegations, ranging from $15,000 to $20,000. Key pricing variables include Saudi GACA permit fees (mandatory for all charters into Saudi Arabia), aircraft positioning within the Gulf, and demand around Saudi Aramco's corporate travel cycles.
Which Airports Are Used for Private Jet Flights Between Abu Dhabi and Dammam?+
From Abu Dhabi, Al Bateen Executive Airport (AZI / OMAD) is the preferred private departure hub; Zayed International Airport (AUH / OMAA) is available for larger aircraft. In Dammam, the gateway is King Fahd International Airport (DMM / OEDF), one of the world's largest airports by total area (780 km²), located approximately 31 km north-west of downtown Dammam in the Eastern Province of Saudi Arabia. Opened in 1999, it is a hub for Flynas and Flyadeal, and the primary gateway for Saudi Aramco and Eastern Province operations. The airport operates 24 hours a day with dedicated private aviation handling and VIP services.
Which Aircraft Models Are Recommended for Private Jet Flights from Abu Dhabi to Dammam?+
The Abu Dhabi, Dammam sector (430 km) is one of the shortest international routes in the Gulf, ideal for light and midsize jets:

1. Phenom 300E (Light Jet) , The world's most popular light jet; covers the sector in approximately 35 to 40 minutes; up to 8 passengers; highly cost-efficient for Gulf hops.
2. Citation CJ3+ (Light Jet) , Reliable, efficient, and proven on Gulf regional routes; excellent short-field performance.
3. Citation XLS+ (Midsize Jet) , For larger delegations; greater cabin space for business meetings or in-flight working.

A dedicated Private Aviation Advisor will match the right aircraft.
What Is the Typical Flight Duration for a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Dammam?+
The flight time between Abu Dhabi (AZI / AUH) and King Fahd International Airport (DMM) is approximately 35 to 45 minutes on a light or midsize jet , one of the shortest international private jet flight times. Departing from Al Bateen, passengers arrive at the FBO just 10 to 15 minutes before departure. On arrival at DMM, VIP handlers ensure rapid Saudi customs and immigration clearance. Downtown Dammam and Khobar are approximately 35 minutes from the airport; Saudi Aramco's Dhahran campus approximately 25 minutes.
Are There Night Flight Restrictions at King Fahd International Airport?+
King Fahd International Airport (DMM / OEDF) operates 24 hours a day with no night-flight curfew, providing complete scheduling flexibility. All charter flights into Saudi Arabia require Saudi GACA prior permission, which our operations team files as standard for every booking. Al Bateen Executive Airport in Abu Dhabi is similarly unrestricted.
How Can I Arrange Ground Transfers After Landing at Dammam Airport?+
We coordinate premium chauffeured transfers , Mercedes S-Class, BMW 7 Series, or armoured vehicles on request , from the private handling ramp. Saudi Aramco's Dhahran headquarters is approximately 25 minutes; downtown Dammam and the Corniche approximately 35 minutes; and Al Khobar's business district approximately 40 minutes. For guests attending meetings at Aramco's compound or at the Dhahran Hills Hotel, we pre-arrange ramp-to-meeting precision timing.
What Is the Luggage Capacity on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Dammam?+
On a light jet such as the Phenom 300E, the aft hold accommodates 4 to 6 bags plus cabin bags. On a midsize jet, capacity is 6 to 8 bags. For this short Gulf hop, luggage is typically minimal; our advisors confirm specifications at booking.
Can My Pets Travel with Me on a Private Jet from Abu Dhabi to Dammam?+
Yes , your cats and dogs can travel in the cabin with you. For travel from the UAE into Saudi Arabia, pets require a valid veterinary health certificate, rabies vaccination records, microchip documentation, and may require a Saudi Ministry of Environment import permit. Our ground team provides a full briefing and coordinates with DMM handlers for any arrival veterinary formalities.
