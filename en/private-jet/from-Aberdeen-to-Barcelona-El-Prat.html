How much does it cost to charter a private jet from Aberdeen to Barcelona?+
Charter pricing on the Aberdeen–Barcelona route typically ranges from €10,000 to €22,000 for a light or midsize jet, making it one of the most accessible direct private aviation routes from Scotland to southern Europe. Pricing variables include aircraft category and group size, seasonal demand peaks (Mobile World Congress in February–March, the Barcelona Grand Prix in May, and summer yacht-season peaks in July–August), aircraft positioning costs from Scotland, landing and handling fees at Barcelona El Prat's business aviation terminal, in-flight catering specifications, and same-day versus multi-day trip pricing. For larger groups requiring super-midsize or large-cabin aircraft, rates rise to €16,000 to €30,000. Fly with Andreea provides fully itemised, transparent quotations.
What airports serve private jet flights between Aberdeen and Barcelona?+
Aberdeen Airport (ABZ / EGPD) handles UK departures with a general aviation facility and private customs. Barcelona–El Prat Airport (BCN / LEBL) handles private jet movements through its dedicated general aviation terminal, with FBO services provided by operators including Execujet and TAG Aviation, offering private lounges, customs facilitation, ramp-side vehicle access, and direct apron transfers. BCN is located 13 km south-west of Barcelona's city centre and provides straightforward private transfer access to the Passeig de Gràcia, Port Vell, and the Eixample district. For clients specifically targeting the Costa Brava or Girona, Girona–Costa Brava Airport (GRO / LEGE) is approximately 100 km north of BCN and offers a faster overland transfer to the northern Costa Brava coastline. Your Fly with Andreea advisor will recommend the optimal airport for your specific Barcelona and Catalan itinerary.
Which private jets are most popular on the Aberdeen to Barcelona route?+
At 1,900 km, the Aberdeen–Barcelona sector sits comfortably within midsize and super-midsize range, with three aircraft dominating bookings in 2025. The Embraer Phenom 300E leads demand on Spain-bound routes from Scotland, combining a nine-passenger cabin, best-in-class range of 3,650 km, and excellent performance into BCN's busy airfield. The Cessna Citation Longitude is the most-requested super-midsize aircraft on this sector, with a stand-up cabin, 2,700 km range, and class-leading noise levels ideal for a two-hour flight. For groups of three to five seeking a cost-efficient option, the Hawker 900XP or Cessna Citation XLS+ provide proven, capable cabins well within the route's range requirements. What is the typical private jet flight time from Aberdeen to Barcelona?+
Aberdeen to Barcelona El Prat covers approximately 1,900 km, with typical block times of two hours ten minutes to two hours forty minutes for light and midsize jets routing via France. Passengers using Aberdeen's private FBO arrive 15 to 20 minutes before departure no shared check-in, no public security lanes, no commercial boarding process. At Barcelona El Prat's business aviation terminal, private customs clearance takes under 10 minutes, with chauffeur vehicles at the aircraft steps for direct transfer to the Passeig de Gràcia (20 minutes), the Port Vell marina (25 minutes), or the W Barcelona Hotel on the beach (30 minutes).
Are there night curfews or operational restrictions at Barcelona El Prat for private jets?+
Barcelona–El Prat Airport (BCN) operates 24 hours a day with no formal published night curfew for general aviation, making it highly flexible for evening arrivals and early-morning departures. Business jet movements are accommodated around the clock through the general aviation terminal, subject to ATC availability and handling-agent confirmation for movements after midnight. Aberdeen Airport (ABZ) has no equivalent night curfew for business aviation. Fly with Andreea's operations team books handling slots, coordinates FBO services, and monitors BCN apron availability particularly during peak periods such as MWC (February–March) and the Barcelona Grand Prix (May), when private jet demand at BCN surges and early slot reservation is essential.

Can my dog or cat fly with me on a private jet from Aberdeen to Barcelona?+
Absolutely your beloved companions travel in the cabin with you, not in cargo, for this two-hour flight to Catalonia. Spain, as an EU member state, applies the EU Pet Travel Scheme for animals arriving from the UK. Required documentation includes: an animal health certificate (AHC) completed by a UK Official Veterinarian (OV) within 10 days of travel; a valid microchip conforming to ISO 11784/11785; a current rabies vaccination administered at least 21 days before first EU entry for previously unvaccinated animals; and for dogs, an Echinococcus (tapeworm) treatment certificate issued between 24 and 120 hours before arrival. Barcelona El Prat's general aviation terminal processes private-flight pet arrivals efficiently. Fly with Andreea will provide the current AHC template and connect you with an OV in your area. We recommend initiating documentation at least 72 hours before departure.
