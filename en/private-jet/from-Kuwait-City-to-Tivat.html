How Much Does It Cost to Charter a Private Jet from Kuwait City to Tivat?+
Chartering a private jet from Kuwait City to Tivat (TIV) typically costs between approximately EUR 30,000 and EUR 85,000 one-way, with a heavy jet starting from around EUR 32,400. Cost moves with aircraft positioning, airport handling, and peak-season demand. When the time is right, Fly With Andreea will see to every detail.
Which Airports Are Used for Private Jet Flights Between Kuwait City and Tivat?+
From Kuwait City, Kuwait City KWI (OKBK) offers full international handling with a dedicated business aviation terminal, approximately 16 km from the centre. In Tivat, the gateway is Tivat TIV (LYTV), approximately 4 km out, with seasonal general aviation handling on the Bay of Kotor. Private operations are handled through dedicated FBO services, with the Montenegrin handling network managed as standard. Our advisors at Fly With Andreea are glad to handle the details whenever you are ready.

What Is the Typical Flight Duration for a Private Jet from Kuwait City to Tivat?+
The Kuwait City (KWI) to Tivat (TIV) block time is approximately 4 hours 2 minutes to 4 hours 45 minutes.
