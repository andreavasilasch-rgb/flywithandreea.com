The Diamond D-Jet was a five-seat single-engine personal jet developed from 2006, prized for its sleek composite airframe and low operating costs. Although the programme did not reach series production, the D-Jet remains a notable step in the personal-jet story.

Why charter it

The case for the D-Jet.

A genuine turn of pace

The D-Jet cruises around 315 knots near Mach 0.55, and on a busy light jet charter that pace routinely saves an hour or more against slower types, time returned at both ends of the day. High cruise also lets the crew climb above weather and airline traffic for a smoother ride. Speed is the one luxury nobody can refund once a day is spent, and the aircraft protects it.

What sets it apart

An insider point: Diamond built the D-Jet almost entirely from carbon composite around a single centreline engine, aiming to make jet flying as forgiving as a high-performance single. That character is part of what you are chartering, not just a cabin.

Sensible economics

On value the D-Jet makes a strong case, turning modest operating costs into a lower hourly rate than clients expect for this much aircraft. Empty-leg and off-peak availability can drop the price further when the diary allows. Pound for pound, this light jet is hard to argue with on a charter budget.

Room where it matters

Step aboard and the D-Jet answers the question every charter client really asks, which is whether the cabin works: up to 5 guests, 30 cubic feet of luggage and seating you can reconfigure for a board meeting, a family or a couple who want the aircraft to themselves. The interior measuring 4 ft 9 in wide by 13 ft 0 in long stays quiet at altitude because the pressurisation holds a low cabin altitude, so the type leaves you fresher than a comparable airline leg. That is the difference private jet charter is really bought for.

Built to depart on time

Behind the comfort sits real engineering: the D-Jet runs 1x Williams FJ33-5A (1,900 lbf), a combination trusted across thousands of charter operations to keep departures punctual. Well-understood systems make it forgiving to maintain and quick to turn around between legs. Dependability, in the end, is the luxury clients feel most on this light jet.

Precision up front

Avionics matter more than passengers realise, and the D-Jet is well found, with Garmin G1000 giving the crew the tools for confident approaches into short, high or busy airports. Automation trims workload, and a rested crew flies a smoother, more punctual charter. That margin is part of why this light jet dispatches so reliably.

The honest view

Where the D-Jet asks a compromise on charter:

Range with a natural ceiling

The D-Jet is honest about distance: near 1350 nautical miles it is superb across its home region, but the longest intercontinental legs call for a bigger tank, and on those we will match you to a larger jet rather than sell you a fuel stop.

If outright top speed or the newest cabin technology is your single priority, a more recent design may edge it, yet for most travellers the D-Jet balance of comfort, capability and value is the more meaningful advantage on the day.

As alternatives to the D-Jet, it is worth weighing the Cirrus Vision SF50 and the Embraer Phenom 100, both able aircraft in the same bracket. Broadly the Cirrus Vision SF50 carries the longer legs, while the D-Jet holds the lower operating cost per hour, so the right pick comes down to your exact route, party and budget, which our advisors are glad to lay out side by side.

All told the D-Jet remains one of the smarter ways to fly short-haul and regional charter, and Fly with Andreea handles catering, transfers, pets and every detail around you, quoting one firm price and replying within the half hour.

D-Jet, your questions answered

How much does it cost to charter the Diamond D-Jet?+
Charter pricing for the Diamond D-Jet typically runs from around EUR 1,800 to 3,000 per flight hour, with the final figure shaped by your routing, dates, aircraft positioning and ground time. As a turboprop it sits in the corresponding bracket, and in our experience arranging Diamond D-Jet charters a well-timed one-way or off-peak booking is often far more competitive than clients expect. Share your route and we return a firm, all-in price within 30 minutes.
What is the range of the Diamond D-Jet, and which routes can it fly nonstop?+
The Diamond D-Jet has a range of roughly 1,350 nautical miles (2,500 km), enough to fly Geneva to Ibiza, direct without a fuel stop. In real operations we plan a little conservatively, since headwinds and a full cabin trim usable range, but on the sectors our clients fly most the Diamond D-Jet reliably turns a two-stop itinerary into a single, seamless hop.
How many passengers can the Diamond D-Jet carry?+
The Diamond D-Jet seats up to 5 guests. For longer flights we usually suggest booking a seat or two below the maximum, which frees space to spread out, work and sleep, and we configure every cabin to the party, whether that is a family, a board or a couple who simply want the whole aircraft to themselves.
How fast is the Diamond D-Jet, and how long do typical flights take?+
The Diamond D-Jet cruises at about 315 knots (583 km/h), around Mach 0.55. In practice that is roughly 2h 01m over 1,000 km and about 7h 10m over 4,000 km. Speed is where this aircraft quietly earns its keep, routinely saving our clients an hour or more against slower types, time handed back at either end of the day.
What is the cabin of the Diamond D-Jet like?+
Step aboard the Diamond D-Jet and the first impression is space and quiet: roughly 4 ft 3 in of headroom, a cabin pressurised and insulated so conversation stays easy and engine noise fades into the background. We find guests consistently step off a Diamond D-Jet flight noticeably fresher than they would from a comparable commercial trip.
How much luggage can the Diamond D-Jet carry?+
The Diamond D-Jet offers about 30 cubic feet of baggage volume, comfortably around 6 large suitcases plus soft bags, and in our experience it swallows skis, golf clubs and a pram without drama. We confirm the exact hold capacity for your specific aircraft and party at the time of booking.
Which airports can the Diamond D-Jet use?+
As a turboprop, the Diamond D-Jet can reach a very wide spread of airports, including shorter, more convenient runways closer to your final destination than the large hubs. We check runway length, altitude and slot availability for every departure and arrival so the aircraft lands as near to where you are going as possible.
On which frequent routes is the Diamond D-Jet chartered?+
The Diamond D-Jet is chartered on a wide spread of city pairs. Popular capital-to-capital routes well within its 1,350 nm reach include Sofia to Athens, Rome to Prague, Sofia to London and Sofia to Oslo. We fly it on hundreds of other sectors too, so tell us your route and we will confirm the aircraft and a firm all-in price.
How does the Diamond D-Jet compare to other turboprops?+
Against other turboprops such as the Cirrus Vision SF50 and the Embraer Phenom 100, the Diamond D-Jet stands out for its balance of range, cabin comfort and real-world reliability. There is rarely a single best jet, only the best jet for a given route and party, and our advisors are happy to lay the options side by side so you can choose on the facts.
Is the Diamond D-Jet a good choice for short-haul and regional flights?+
Absolutely. The Diamond D-Jet is purpose-made for short-haul and regional flying of up to around 1,350 nautical miles, slipping into smaller, closer airfields and keeping costs efficient. For longer journeys we would match you to a larger, longer-range jet.
What engines power the Diamond D-Jet, and how reliable is it?+
The Diamond D-Jet is powered by 1x Williams FJ33-5A (1,900 lbf). It runs the Garmin G1000 avionics suite. It is a mature, well-supported design, and reliability is exactly why we place clients on it, because the smoothest charter is the one that departs on time and arrives without a second thought.
Can I bring my pets on board the Diamond D-Jet?+
Yes. Pets fly in the cabin beside you on the Diamond D-Jet, never in a hold, and in our experience this is one of the main reasons families choose private in the first place. We brief you well ahead on microchip, vaccination and paperwork for your specific route so boarding is effortless.
At what altitude does the Diamond D-Jet cruise, and why does that matter?+
The Diamond D-Jet cruises as high as 25,000 feet. Flying above most weather and commercial traffic means a smoother, quieter ride and more direct routings, and a lower effective cabin altitude is a large part of why guests arrive feeling rested rather than drained.
Is there a more affordable way to charter the Diamond D-Jet?+
Often, yes. Empty-leg and one-way pricing on the Diamond D-Jet can be a fraction of a standard round trip when your dates are flexible, and off-peak departures help too. We watch live availability and flag genuine turboprop opportunities the moment they appear.
