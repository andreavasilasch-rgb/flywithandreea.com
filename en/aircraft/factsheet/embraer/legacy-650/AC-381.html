At a glance

This aircraft is an Embraer Legacy 650, a heavy jet built in 2015. It seats up to 13 passengers with 24 suitcases and flies up to 7,223 km (3,900 nm) non-stop. Its high-speed cruise is 850 km/h.

Step on board and the cabin opens out: 15.19 m long, 2.11 m wide and 1.83 m high, with seats for 13. This aircraft was built in 2015. 24 suitcases travel with you in the baggage compartment, about 8.2 m³ of space.

In the air

In the air the Legacy 650 flies up to 7,223 km without a stop. Its high-speed cruise is 850 km/h.
