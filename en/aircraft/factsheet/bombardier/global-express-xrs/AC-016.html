At a glance

This aircraft is a Bombardier Global Express XRS, an ultra long range jet built in 2003. It seats up to 12 passengers with 16 suitcases and flies up to 11,390 km (6,150 nm) non-stop. Its high-speed cruise is 933 km/h.

Stepping on board

Step on board and the cabin opens out: 13.18 m long, 2.41 m wide and 1.88 m high, tall enough to stand upright, with seats for 12. This aircraft was built in 2003 and refurbished in 2023. Sixteen suitcases travel with you in the baggage compartment, about 5.4 m³ of space.

In the air

In the air the Global Express XRS flies up to 11,390 km without a stop. Its high-speed cruise is 933 km/h. Satellite Wi-Fi keeps you connected. A full galley with an espresso machine serves the cabin. A flight attendant joins the flight on request. There is an enclosed lavatory on board. Your pet travels in the cabin with you.

Journeys such as Riga to Paris, Vilnius to New York or Riga to Aspen.
