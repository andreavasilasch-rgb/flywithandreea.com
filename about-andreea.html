Good to know

Ask Andreea.

Straight answers, in my own words.

Am I really dealing with just one person?+

Yes. When you message Fly with Andreea, you reach me directly. I source your aircraft, confirm every detail and stay reachable if anything changes on the day. No call centre, no shared inbox, no handovers.

How can I pay for my charter?+

You can pay by credit or debit card or by bank transfer, and on some occasions by cryptocurrency. I set out the available options clearly when I send your quote.

Which currency do you charter in?+

Mainly US dollars and euros. That said, depending on the route and the aircraft, almost any currency can be accepted. Tell me what works best for you and I will arrange it.

How long is my quote valid?+

Anywhere from 24 hours up to a month, depending on the type of flight and how far ahead you are booking. I always tell you exactly how long your price is held.

Can you arrange multi leg trips?+

Of course. Several stops across several cities, planned as one itinerary with one point of contact. I build the whole sequence around your schedule so each leg connects smoothly.

Can you reach hard to reach destinations?+

If another aircraft can fly there, so can you. Some destinations simply need specific arrangements, permits or handling, and I take care of all of it before you travel.

Can you obtain special permits?+

Often, yes. I have organised many flights that required special permits for all sorts of reasons. Very little is genuinely impossible, so it is always worth asking.

Can I fly with cargo or valuables?+

Yes. I will send you the list of documents required to proceed with the flight, then handle the arrangements with the operator so everything clears properly.

Can my pet or live animals travel?+

Very often, yes. It is one of the most common requests I receive. Depending on the destination and the breed, it can almost always be settled, in the cabin beside you where possible.

What about last minute or complicated routes?+

This is where I am at my best. When a route looks impossible or the timing is tight, that is usually my favourite kind of request. I find the solution that others miss.

What if another broker has already said no?+

Ask me anyway. The requests other brokers decline are frequently the ones I find easy. There is usually a way, and finding it is my job.

How does the broker model work, and is my booking safe?+

Fly with Andreea is an air charter broker. I arrange your flight with third party, AOC certified operators from a network of more than 450 vetted operators, and I confirm pricing in writing before you commit. I manage the operator relationship on your behalf from start to finish.

Where can you fly me?+

Across Europe, the Middle East and Africa, with around 96 countries within reach, and worldwide on request. If you do not see your route anywhere on the site, send it to me and I will arrange it.
