How does the air charter broker model work, and is my booking safe?+

Fly with Andreea is an air charter broker. Your flight is arranged with third-party, AOC-certified operators drawn from a network of more than 450 vetted operators, with pricing confirmed in writing before you commit. Fly with Andreea manages the operator relationship on your behalf from start to finish and does not own or operate aircraft. The full contractual position is set out in the terms of service.

What is the difference between air charter services and an aircraft operator?+

An operator holds the Air Operator Certificate, owns or manages the aircraft, employs the crew and is the party that actually flies you. Air charter services sit on the client side: they hold no aircraft, so they can search the whole market rather than sell one fleet. Fly with Andreea is an air charter broker in that second group, which is why the recommendation can change from one operator to another between two trips on the same route.

Is Fly with Andreea independent of the operators it books?+

Yes. Fly with Andreea has no ownership link to any operator and no head office setting a preferred list, so each aircraft is chosen for the trip in front of it. The whole fleet is open, from turboprops and light jets through to heavy jets and VIP airliners, and the recommendation follows from what the route genuinely needs rather than from who is easiest to book.

How should I compare air charter services before booking?+

Five things separate air charter services, and all five can be asked before you pay anything. Does the quote name the operator, the aircraft type and the registration, or only a category? Is the price written and itemised, so positioning, handling and taxes are visible? Will the same person handle the booking from enquiry to landing? Is the operator's AOC checked for your specific flight? And is the quote free of obligation? Fly with Andreea answers yes to all five, and any broker worth using should be willing to do the same in writing.

Can you arrange multi leg trips?+

Of course. Several stops across several cities, planned as one itinerary with one point of contact. Fly with Andreea builds the whole sequence around your schedule so each leg connects smoothly, and positions the aircraft between stops where that is cheaper than releasing and re-sourcing it.

What is an empty leg flight?+

An empty leg, also called a repositioning flight, is an aircraft that has to fly a sector anyway, either to collect its next passengers or to return to base. The cabin is empty, so the same aircraft and the same crew can be chartered for a fraction of the usual rate. The trade is flexibility: the date and the route are set by the operator rather than by you.

How often are the empty legs updated?+

Daily. The empty legs page is refreshed every day with live repositioning flights, and sectors appear and disappear within hours as operators confirm their next bookings. If a leg on the list matters to you, it is worth asking the same day rather than the same week.

What if there is no empty leg on my route?+

Most routes have none on any given day, which is normal. Tell Fly with Andreea the sector and the rough dates and it will be watched as new repositioning flights are loaded. In the meantime a standard charter quote is prepared, so you always have a confirmed option rather than only a maybe.

Will I deal with the same person throughout?+

Yes. At Fly with Andreea your enquiry stays with one named contact, who sources your aircraft, confirms every detail and remains reachable if anything changes on the day. A small team works alongside that person and already knows your trip, so it is always covered without being handed to somebody who has to be brought up to speed.

Is someone reachable outside office hours?+

Yes. Charter does not keep office hours and neither does Fly with Andreea. Your named contact is reachable by phone, WhatsApp and email seven days a week, including while your flight is in the air. A request that arrives at two in the morning is answered at two in the morning, because that is usually when it matters most.

Is my flight monitored once it is booked?+

Yes. Fly with Andreea tracks the aircraft from the moment it is confirmed, including its positioning flight in, and watches anything that could move your departure: weather, airspace restrictions, slot changes, crew timings. You are told before it becomes a problem rather than after. Once you have landed, the final times and any variations are confirmed back to you in writing.

How are operators vetted?+

Every flight is arranged with operators holding a current Air Operator Certificate from their national authority. Fly with Andreea checks that the certificate is valid for the type of flight being asked for, that the aircraft is on that operator's certificate, and that the insurance in place is appropriate to the trip. Operators that cannot evidence all three do not get the booking, however attractive the price.

Will you ever book the cheapest aircraft regardless?+

No. Price is one input. An aircraft that cannot make the sector without a fuel stop, a crew close to their duty limit, or an operator whose paperwork does not stand up are all reasons to recommend something else and explain why. Where a cheaper option is genuinely equivalent, it is the one put in front of you.

What happens if the aircraft goes technical?+

The operator is responsible for providing the flight, and Fly with Andreea manages that relationship for you, which in practice means sourcing a replacement rather than passing on a phone number. Because the same contact has handled your trip from the first message, nothing has to be explained again while a solution is found.

What payment options are accepted?+

Bank transfer, credit card and debit card, and on some occasions cryptocurrency. Corporate accounts can be settled by invoice where terms have been agreed in advance. The available options for your specific flight are set out clearly with your quote, because what an operator will accept varies with the route and the lead time.

Which currencies can I pay in?+

Four currencies: US dollars (USD), Euros (EUR), Pounds sterling (GBP) and UAE Dirhams (AED). Most operators quote in USD, EUR or GBP, and AED is settled routinely from Dubai. Tell Fly with Andreea which of the four suits you and the quote is issued in it, with the rate fixed at the point of quoting rather than floating until payment.

How is the price put together, and are there hidden fees?+

The quote is a single written figure covering the aircraft, crew, fuel, landing and handling, and it states what is included before you commit. Positioning flights, overnight crew costs, de-icing, peak-period surcharges and any applicable taxes are shown rather than added later. Knowing where costs usually hide is most of the job, and an avoidable handling fee spotted at quote stage is money that stays with you.

Is my money safe before the flight operates?+

Charter is paid before departure, so it is a fair question to ask of any broker. Fly with Andreea Business Jets FZCO is a licensed UAE company, funds for your flight are passed to the operating carrier for that specific flight, and your written confirmation names the operator, the aircraft and the registration before anything is paid. If a flight cannot be operated, the position on refunds is set out in the terms of service rather than decided afterwards.

Does a quote commit me to anything?+

No. Quotes from Fly with Andreea are free and carry no obligation. There is no account to open, no membership to buy and no minimum spend, and asking what a route costs is not the start of a sales sequence.

How long is my quote valid?+

Anywhere from 24 hours up to a month, depending on the type of flight and how far ahead you are booking. Empty legs are the short end of that range and can move within hours. Every Fly with Andreea quote states exactly how long the price is held.

What are the cancellation terms?+

Cancellation terms are set by the operator and vary with the aircraft and how close to departure you are. They are stated in writing with the quote, before anything is signed, so the position is known in advance rather than discovered later.

How quickly can a flight be arranged?+

Popular sectors are often confirmed the same day, some in as little as 40 minutes. Others need 48 hours, usually because of permits, slots or crew duty time rather than aircraft availability. Fly with Andreea will say which of the two your request is when you first ask, rather than after you have waited.

Can you arrange an urgent or same-day departure?+

Often, yes. Same-day and within-the-hour departures are among the most common requests Fly with Andreea receives, and from Dubai the Gulf, Europe, Africa and the Indian subcontinent are all reachable at short notice. What sets the limit is almost never the aircraft: it is permits, airport slots and crew duty time. Those are checked first, so you are given a realistic departure time rather than an optimistic one.

Which air charter services are available from Dubai at short notice?+

From Dubai, Fly with Andreea can usually arrange private jet charter, group charter, air ambulance and cargo at short notice across the Gulf, Europe, Africa and the Indian subcontinent, with VIP airliners on longer lead times because of crew and slot planning. Can my pet travel in the cabin?+

Very often, yes, and it is one of the most common requests Fly with Andreea receives. Depending on the destination and the breed it can almost always be settled, with the animal in the cabin beside you where the operator and the arrival country allow it. Import rules, not the aircraft, are usually the limiting factor, and they are checked before anything is confirmed.

Can you carry cargo, valuables or obtain special permits?+

Yes to all three. For cargo and valuables you will be sent the list of documents required to proceed, and the arrangements are then handled with the operator so everything clears properly. Fly with Andreea has organised many flights needing special overflight or landing permits, for all sorts of reasons. Very little is genuinely impossible, so it is always worth asking.

What if another broker has already said no?+

Ask Fly with Andreea anyway. Routes that look impossible, tight timings and requests other brokers decline are frequently the straightforward ones here. There is usually a way, and finding it is the job.
