What is an empty leg flight?
An empty leg is a private jet flying one way with no passengers, usually to reposition for its next booking or to return to base. That otherwise-empty flight is offered at a reduced price, so you get a full private charter for a fraction of the usual cost.
How do empty leg flights work?
When a charter drops clients in one city, the aircraft often has to fly empty to its next pickup. We list those repositioning flights here. You pick a leg that matches your route and dates, message us, and we confirm the aircraft, timing and final price.
How much can I save on an empty leg?
Savings vary by route, aircraft and timing, but empty legs are typically 25 to 75% below the standard one-way charter price. The closer the departure and the better the aircraft fit, the larger the saving.
Are empty leg flights cheaper than first or business class?
They can be. For small groups travelling together, an empty leg sometimes costs little more than several premium commercial tickets, while giving you a private cabin, your own schedule and a private terminal. Send us the route and party size for a same-day comparison.
How do I book an empty leg?
Tap Ask on WhatsApp on any leg, or request a quote. The message is pre-filled with the route, date and price, so you only confirm. We reply quickly and hold the aircraft on a first come, first served basis.
How far in advance should I book an empty leg?
Empty legs appear from a few days to a few weeks before departure and move fast. If you see one that fits, enquire immediately; if not, set up an alert and we will notify you the moment a matching leg is released.
Why do empty leg prices and times change?
Empty legs depend on the original charter that creates them. If that flight shifts, the date, time or availability of the empty leg can change. Prices shown are indicative and confirmed in a written quote before you commit.
What happens if the empty leg is cancelled or the dates move?
Because an empty leg follows a paying charter, it can be rescheduled or withdrawn if that flight changes. If yours moves, we tell you immediately and either rebook you on another leg, arrange a tailored charter, or there is no charge.
How many passengers can fly on an empty leg?
It depends on the aircraft, from around four seats on a very light jet up to a dozen or more on a heavy or long-range jet. Each card shows the seat count, and we confirm the exact cabin when you enquire.
Can I book a one-way private jet with an empty leg?
Yes. Empty legs are one-way by nature, which is exactly why they are priced so well. If you need the return too, we can quote a separate empty leg or a standard charter for the way back.
How much luggage can I bring on an empty leg?
Luggage depends on the aircraft and the number of passengers, but private cabins are generous compared with commercial flights. Tell us your bags, ski or golf equipment when you enquire and we confirm the hold capacity for that specific jet.
Can pets travel on an empty leg flight?
In most cases yes. Pets usually travel in the cabin beside you rather than in cargo. Let us know the animal and size when you message and we will confirm with the operator and handle the paperwork.
Can I change the date, time or route of an empty leg?
Empty legs follow a fixed repositioning, so the route and timing are largely set. Small adjustments are sometimes possible; if it does not fit, we will find another leg or arrange a tailored charter at a custom price.
What aircraft are used for empty legs?
Everything from very light and light jets for short hops to super-midsize, heavy and long-range jets for longer routes. You can filter the list by aircraft category, and each card names the exact aircraft and cabin.
Are empty leg flights safe?
Yes. Empty legs use the same vetted operators, aircraft and crews as full-price charters, held to the same safety and maintenance standards. The only difference is the price, not the standard of flying.
How do I pay for an empty leg, and is a deposit required?
Once we confirm availability we send a written quote and a secure payment link. We will never ask for card details over chat. Payment terms depend on the operator and timing, and we confirm them before you commit.
Can I get an empty leg from Dubai or my home city?
Often, yes. Empty legs appear worldwide, including to and from Dubai and the wider Gulf. Use the search and region filters, or set up an alert for your city and we will message you when a matching leg is listed.
What is the difference between an empty leg and a regular charter?
A regular charter is built around your exact route and schedule at full price. An empty leg is an existing repositioning flight you join at a discount, trading some flexibility on route and timing for a much lower price.
Do empty legs include catering and ground transfers?
Light catering and chauffeur transfers can usually be arranged on request, just as with a full charter. Mention what you need when you enquire and we will coordinate it around your flight.
How do I get notified about new empty leg flights?
Tap Set up empty-leg alerts and tell us your usual routes. We will message you the moment a matching empty leg is released, so you can grab it before it is gone.
