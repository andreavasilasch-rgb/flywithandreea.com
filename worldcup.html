Private jets to the World Cup. Answered.

Everything you need to know about flying private to the 2026 matches. If your question is not here, we are one message away.

Can you fly me to a specific World Cup match city on short notice?
Yes. With 450+ operators worldwide and a 55-minute minimum take-off, we routinely arrange same-day and next-day flights to the host cities across the USA, Canada and Mexico. Message us the fixture and we confirm an aircraft fast.
Which airports serve the World Cup 2026 host cities?
Every host city has strong private-aviation options, often several. New York (Teterboro), Los Angeles (Van Nuys), Miami (Opa-locka), Dallas, Atlanta, Toronto, Vancouver, Mexico City and more. We pick the airport closest to your stadium and hotel and handle the slots.
How far in advance should I book a jet for a World Cup match?
Booking two to three days ahead gives the widest choice of aircraft, especially around big fixtures when demand spikes. That said, we can often confirm within a few hours when an aircraft is positioned nearby.
Can you arrange a same-day return after a match?
Absolutely. Fly in for kick-off and back out the same night. We plan crew duty and slots around the final whistle, including extra time, so a late finish never leaves you stranded.
Can a group or corporate box travel together on one jet?
Yes. From a midsize cabin for a small party to a VIP airliner for a full corporate group, we match the aircraft to your numbers so everyone flies together, on your schedule.
What does a private jet to a World Cup match cost?
Charter is priced by the trip: aircraft category, distance, positioning and airport fees. A short domestic hop on a light jet starts in the low thousands; larger cabins and longer legs rise from there. We quote all-in with no hidden fees.
Can I follow my team through the knockout rounds on private jets?
Yes, and many clients do. Tell us your team and we build a flexible plan that moves with the bracket, city to city, all the way to the final on 19 July, adjusting as fixtures are confirmed.
Do you handle travel across all three host countries?
Yes. The 2026 tournament spans the USA, Canada and Mexico. We coordinate cross-border private flights, customs and immigration at private terminals, so you move between countries smoothly.
Can I bring flags, banners and fan gear on board?
Of course. Private cabins are generous with space, and there is no commercial baggage queue. Bring your flags, drums and colours, we will make sure it all travels with you.
How close to the stadium can we land?
Usually very close. Most host cities have a dedicated business-aviation airport minutes from the stadium, and we arrange the ground transfer so you go from cabin to your seat with no queues.
Can you coordinate ground transfers from the airport to the stadium?
Yes. Door-to-tarmac and tarmac-to-stadium transfers are part of the service, chauffeured cars, or a helicopter into the city centre on match day when time is tight.
Can my pet travel with me to the games?
In most cases, yes. Well-behaved pets fly in the cabin beside you rather than in cargo. We confirm the entry requirements for each host country in advance and handle the paperwork.
What happens if the match time changes or goes to extra time?
We build in flexibility. Because you are on a private schedule, a later kick-off, extra time or penalties simply shifts your departure, we hold the aircraft and re-time the slot for you.
Can you arrange a helicopter transfer into the city on match day?
Yes. From the private terminal we can connect a helicopter straight into the city centre or close to the stadium, bypassing match-day traffic entirely.
Do you offer empty-leg deals around the tournament?
Yes. Repositioning flights appear throughout the tournament and can be a fraction of standard charter price. Tell us your route and dates and we will alert you the moment a matching empty leg is listed.
Can you fly late at night after evening kick-offs?
Yes. Most host-city private terminals operate around the clock. Evening kick-offs and late finishes are no problem, we confirm operating hours and crew duty for your exact fixture.
How many passengers can travel together?
Anywhere from four on a very light jet to fifty on a VIP airliner. Tell us your group size and luggage and we match the right cabin, so nobody is left behind.
Can you cater a celebration on board after a win?
Yes. Champagne, your preferred catering and a cabin set for the occasion, we arrange it in advance so the celebration starts the moment you board.
Do I need a visa to travel between host countries?
Requirements depend on your nationality and the countries you are crossing. Share your passport details early and we will confirm what you need for the USA, Canada or Mexico before you fly.
How do I book a jet for a specific fixture?
Tap “Book a flight for this match” on any fixture card, it opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message with the game, date and time. Add your passenger count and luggage, send it, and Andreea takes care of the rest.
