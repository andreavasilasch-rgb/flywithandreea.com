Private jet empty legs from Dubai, and across Europe.

An empty leg is a private jet already flying with nobody on board, repositioning for its next charter or returning to base. Because the aircraft is going anyway, the flight is sold at a steep discount on the usual charter price, which is what makes a private jet cheaper this way than any other. You take the whole cabin, not a seat, with the same crew and the same private terminals.

It is last minute by definition. An empty leg exists only because somebody else has already booked the aircraft, so these are last minute private jet deals in the literal sense: the date is set, the discount is real, and the flight is gone once it is taken.

What an empty leg costs. On this page the cheapest is €1,450 and the middle of the range is about €2,500, for the entire aircraft rather than a seat. A charter of the same sector booked on your own date would usually be several times that. 47 of the 79 flights carry a price today; the rest are priced on request, because the operator sets the figure when the dates are confirmed.

Questions

Empty leg flights, questions and answers.

What an empty leg is

What is an empty leg flight?

An aircraft that has to fly a sector with nobody on board. Somebody chartered it one way, so it has to get home or on to wherever it is needed next, and that repositioning flight is going whether anyone is in the cabin or not. You take the cabin instead of letting it fly empty.

Why does it cost less?

The cost of the sector is already committed to the charter that caused it. What you pay is set against a flight that was happening anyway, rather than against the full cost of putting an aircraft and a crew in the air for you.

Is the aircraft any different from a normal charter?

No. The same aircraft, the same crew, the same private terminals at both ends, the same handling. Nothing about the flight is reduced. The only real difference is who chose the date.

Do I book a seat or the whole aircraft?

The whole aircraft. No seat sharing and no strangers. The price shown is the total for the aircraft, not a price per person.

Are empty legs worth it?

If your dates can move, yes, and considerably. If your dates cannot move, a normal charter quote is usually the better answer, and Andreea will tell you that rather than sell you a leg that will not hold.

What it costs

How much does an empty leg cost?

It depends on the aircraft and the sector, and the figure on each card is the one the operator has released for that leg. As a rule it sits well below the charter rate for the same aircraft on the same day. Ask for the charter price of the same sector and Andreea will send it, so you can see the difference rather than take it on trust.

How does that compare with a standard charter?

The card shows what the operator has released for the empty leg. It does not show the charter rate beside it, because a charter price is quoted for a particular trip rather than published. Ask on any leg and Andreea will price the same sector on your own dates, which is a real comparison on a single flight rather than a headline figure.

Are taxes and fees included in the price shown?

Not yet. The figure on the card is the aircraft. Taxes, handling and landing fees are confirmed with the written quote, as one fixed all in figure, before you commit to anything.

Why do some legs show no price?

Because the operator has not released one for that sector. The aircraft, the route and the date are real. Ask and Andreea comes back with a figure in writing.

Can the price change after I ask?

The figure on the card is what the operator has released, and it can move if the operator revises it or if the sector changes. Andreea confirms the price in writing before anything is agreed, and that written figure is the one that stands.

Asking for one

How do I book an empty leg?

Open the leg, press Book now, and give Andreea your name, your contact and how many are travelling. She checks with the operator that the leg is still there, then sends you a written quote. Nothing is charged at any point in that.

What happens after I send a request?

Andreea reads it herself. She confirms the leg is still available, puts the price in writing with everything included, and tells you plainly where it stands. If it has already gone she says so and looks for another.

Do I pay anything to ask?

No. Nothing is charged until the flight is confirmed, and you see one fixed all in figure in writing before that point.

Can I ask about a leg that is not on this page?

Yes. Tell Andreea the route and roughly when, and she watches for it. New legs are released continuously and most never reach a public page before they go.

How certain it is

How likely is an empty leg to actually happen?

Less certain than a charter, and it depends mostly on how close the departure is. A leg leaving in a few days is far more settled than one several weeks out, because the charter that caused it is already fixed. Andreea tells you where a particular one stands before you commit.

Can the date or the route of an empty leg change?

Yes. The aircraft belongs to whoever chartered the flight it is repositioning for, so a change to their plans changes this one. That is the honest risk of flying this way, and it is why nothing is charged until the flight is confirmed.

What if it falls away after I have asked for it?

It happens. Nothing is charged, and Andreea looks for another leg on the route or quotes the sector properly so you still travel.

Can I change the date or the time?

The date rarely, because it follows the original charter. The time sometimes, by a few hours, if the operator allows it. Say in your request what would suit you and Andreea will ask.

Can I choose my departure time?

Not usually. The slot is set by the flight the aircraft is repositioning for. Many legs carry some flexibility and the card says so, but it is hours rather than days.

When is the best time to look?

There is no best time, because they appear continuously, some months ahead and some the day before departure. The list on this page changes daily. If you have a route in mind, telling Andreea once is better than watching the page.

On board

How many people can travel?

Up to the seat count on the card, which is the aircraft's own. There is no extra charge for filling the cabin, because you have the whole aircraft either way.

How much luggage can I bring?

Open any leg and the preview gives the number of suitcases the hold takes for that aircraft, where the operator has stated it. If you are travelling with skis, golf clubs or anything awkward, say so in your request so it is checked against that aircraft before anything is confirmed.

Can I bring my dog?

On many of them. Where the operator has confirmed it, the preview says so and the Pets welcome filter brings up only those legs. Pets travel in the cabin beside you rather than in the hold. They need a microchip, a valid rabies vaccination and the right animal health paperwork, and you get a full briefing before departure.

Is there wifi and a flight attendant?

It depends on the aircraft. Open any leg and the preview lists what that one has. Both are also filters, so you can see only the legs the operator has confirmed them on. Wifi is on the aircraft, and the connection itself is billed separately.

Can I book an empty leg for a team or a group?

Yes, up to the seat count. Larger parties are a group charter rather than an empty leg, and Andreea arranges those too.

Airports and safety

Which airports do empty legs use?

The airports the repositioning flight is already using, which are usually the private terminals rather than the main passenger ones. Both ends are named on the card by their codes, so there is no guessing which field it is.

Are empty leg flights safe?

Every flight is operated by a fully vetted, AOC certified carrier, held to the same standard as any other charter. An empty leg is a commercial arrangement, not a different kind of flying.

Who operates the flight?

A certified air carrier. Fly with Andreea is an air charter broker and arranges flights with third party AOC certified operators, and does not own or operate aircraft.

This page

How often is this list updated?

Daily. Legs are added as operators release them and dropped once they have gone or the date has passed. A leg that has already departed never shows.

There is nothing on my route. What now?

Tell Andreea the route and your travel window. She watches for it and writes when one appears. If none does, the route can be quoted as a normal charter on your own dates instead.
